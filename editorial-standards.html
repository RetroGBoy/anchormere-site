How this content is produced — and what we do not claim

Four editorial rules we hold to

四条编辑原则

  • Write per category, not per keyword: the engineering notes, decision scenario, glossary and standard list on each sourcing page are written for that category alone — not produced by swapping a head term.
  • Cite public sources and keep the trail: public cases print their full Source URL in plain text; standards are cited editorially with their scope stated.
  • Label every number: MOQ, lead time, tolerance, wattage and cost figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is confirmed against the drawing, target market and written RFQ.
  • Claim nothing borrowed: public cases are not our client projects, we claim no certification, and we invent no reviews, prices, customer names or staff biographies.

What this site does not claim

我们明确不主张的事

Stating the boundary is part of being credible. The following are current facts, not placeholders waiting to be filled.

  • We do not claim to hold ISO, IEC, UL, CE, FCC or any other certification. Those names appear only as public references a buyer can ask a supplier to evidence.
  • We show no client portfolio, partner list or performance endorsement. Public cases come from reporting and keep their full source URLs.
  • We provide no company certification documents and maintain no reserved section or placeholder for them. Entity information is limited to what is already disclosed in the footer and the Organization entity.
  • We invent no author biographies, named staff or external profile links. The review body is disclosed uniformly as the Anchormere sourcing desk.

The same boundary applies to structured data: the site declares no Product / Offer / aggregateRating retail entities or ratings, so it is not misclassified as an e-commerce product page.

Corrections and feedback

更正与反馈

If a fact, standard scope or typical value does not match its source, send us the page URL and the exact sentence via the contact form. We check it against the cited source, then correct the page or explain why the wording stands.

This page and the rest of the site are checked before every publish by verification gates: visible review lines paired with structured review dates, case source URLs in plain text, standard citations with disclaimers, and no retail-entity misclassification.

FAQ

常见问题

Who writes and reviews this content?

The Anchormere sourcing desk — an in-house sourcing engineering team — writes the category notes and reviews them against public standards, supplier processes and typical buyer RFQs. Each sourcing page carries a visible review date.

Do you hold the certifications you mention?

No. Standards such as IEC, ISO, IPC, FCC, CE and Incoterms are cited as public references for buyers to verify with their chosen supplier. Anchormere claims no certification and represents no issuing body.

Are the case studies your own clients?

No. All public case references come from reporting, official rulings or public filings, each with its full source URL. They are industry references, not Anchormere clients, partners or endorsements.

Are the specifications and figures binding?

No. MOQ, lead times, tolerances, wattage and cost figures are typical industry ranges for orientation. The binding values are those confirmed in writing against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

How do I report an error or request a correction?

Use the contact form, quoting the page URL and the sentence in question. We review the claim against the cited source and either correct the page or explain why it stands.
