Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Why build hardware startups in the Greater Bay Area: a hardware venture lives or dies on iteration speed and cash burn. Shenzhen's cluster puts industrial designers, rapid tooling, PCBA houses, 3D printing and small-batch molders within the same metro, so a prototype can become a crowdfunding-ready batch without a six-month tooling cycle. Anchormere is the coordinator for that journey: we take your NPI from prototype to a 500-piece pilot, protect your IP with controlled drawings and NDAs, and consolidate the build so you present backers with a real, shippable product instead of a render.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can you take a prototype to a crowdfunding-ready batch?

Yes. We run NPI from your prototype to a 500-piece pilot, with rapid tooling and 3D-printed enclosures so you can ship real units to backers.

How do you protect my IP?

We work from controlled drawings under NDA, limit drawing distribution to vetted workshops, and never list your product openly without consent.

What is the minimum for a pilot run?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation is available if your campaign spans several SKUs.

How fast can tooling and first units happen?

Rapid soft tooling and 3D-printed enclosures in 7-15 days; pilot production 20-35 days after sample approval.

Do you support IoT and connected devices?

Yes. We integrate PCBA, wireless modules, enclosures and firmware hand-off, and consolidate finished connected devices into one shipment.
