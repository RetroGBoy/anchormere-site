Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Drone parts are a balance-and-stiffness problem: carbon-fibre layup, CNC tolerances and propeller dynamic balance drive flight behaviour more than raw motor power.

Process & technical notes

  • Carbon-fibre plate layup orientation and resin content are controlled per batch; a layup change alters frame torsional stiffness.
  • CNC motor mounts and arm interfaces are machined to a shared datum so arms remain interchangeable in the field.
  • Propellers are dynamically balanced as a set and matched by weight class before packing.

Buyer checklist

  • Provide the frame drawing with datum scheme, layup spec and the motor mount pattern; tolerances on arm interfaces are flight-critical.
  • Specify whether you need matched propeller sets, spare arms and a documented part-number cross-reference.
  • Clarify regulatory scope for the finished assembly (CE / FCC / drone registration) versus parts-only supply.

QC & inspection focus

  • Dynamic balance and weight-class verification on propeller sets; runout check on motor mounts.
  • Carbon plate thickness and layup record per lot, plus a pull/bend test on sampled arm joints.
