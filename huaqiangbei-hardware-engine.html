Huaqiangbei: the world's hardware prototyping engine

华强北：全球硬件原型开发引擎

On about 1.5 km² in central Shenzhen sits Huaqiangbei — widely described as the world's largest electronics marketplace. For hardware buyers it is the fastest place on earth to turn an idea into a working sample, then into a production run.

At a glance

LocationFutian District, Shenzhen, Guangdong
Footprint~1.45 km²; dozens of specialist electronics markets
Businesses~115,000 operators across the district
ReachGoods flow to ~180–190 countries and regions; thousands of foreign buyers daily
Reputation"China's No. 1 Electronics Street"; Asia's largest electronics distribution hub

The "one kilometer, one week" advantage

Huaqiangbei's edge is not just selection — it is proximity. The local ecosystem is described as "one-kilometer support, one-week mass production": chips, passives, connectors, enclosures, tooling, PCB assembly and software talent sit within walking distance, so a prototype that would take months elsewhere can be iterated in days.

  • Components on the spot — from capacitors and sensors to the newest microcontrollers and modules.
  • Rapid iteration — small workshops and makerspaces turn drawings into samples within a week.
  • Mature cross-border trade — the district has a well-developed export and logistics chain built for small-parcel and consolidated shipping.

Where an integrator fits

Huaqiangbei is phenomenal for discovery and prototyping, but quality and IP discipline vary widely stall-to-stall. Anchormere bridges the gap: we qualify suppliers, lock specifications, run QC, and move a prototype into reliable volume production across the wider GBA — without you gambling on an unknown stall.
