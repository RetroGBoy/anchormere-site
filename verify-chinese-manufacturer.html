How to Verify a Chinese Manufacturer | Factory Audit & Scam Avoidance

A practical checklist to verify a Chinese manufacturer: business license, factory audit, third-party inspection, payment safety and the red flags that signal a scam.

Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Verifying a Chinese manufacturer is the single highest-leverage step in overseas procurement. A real, vetted factory protects your deposit, your IP and your shipment; a fake one or a trading company pretending to be a factory can cost you both money and a season. This guide covers the checks that matter: confirm the operating entity and business license, request a recorded shop-floor video and certificate wall, commission a third-party inspection, and structure payment so you never prepay 100%. How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Supplier verification is evidence, not reassurance: licence, certificate holder, production address, equipment and a live shop-floor walkthrough must all agree.

What we do, step by step

  • Start with the business licence: registered entity, scope, legal representative and whether it matches the bank account name.
  • Check certificates by holder name, issue date and scope — a certificate for a sister company does not cover your order.
  • Confirm the production address, not the registered office, and walk the actual line on a live video call.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Request a by-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video and a live inspection by appointment.
  • Cross-check the quotation entity, the contract party and the deposit account.
  • Write third-party inspection rights and AQL acceptance into the contract before the deposit.

How we verify & report

  • A document wall per project with licence, certificates, test reports and expiry dates visible.
  • Pre-shipment inspection against the signed golden sample with a written report, not a verbal confirmation.

Licence, certificate scope and sample provenance

执照、证书范围与样品溯源

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • Business licence verification checks the registered scope, the legal representative, the registered capital and the operating term — and whether the entity name matches the bank account that will receive the deposit.
  • A quality-system certificate is only meaningful with its scope, issuing body and validity date; 'ISO 9001 certified' without a scope may cover a trading office rather than the production site.
  • On-site or remote verification should confirm the equipment list against the process the supplier claims to run, and check whether key steps are subcontracted to another workshop.
  • Sample provenance matters: ask for a first-article report, the material lot and a serial-linked inspection record, so the approved golden sample can be compared with a later production unit.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer receives a polished factory video and a low quote. A video proves a camera was present, not that the line is theirs; we normally request the licence-bank name match, a certificate with scope, and a live walkthrough of the specific process, which separates a factory from a trading intermediary quickly.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

How do I verify a Chinese manufacturer is real?

Confirm the uniform social credit code on the business license, match it to the operating entity, and request a recorded shop-floor video and certificate wall. A legitimate factory will provide these.

What are the red flags of a scam?

No verifiable business license, refusal to show the factory, pressure to prepay 100%, prices far below market, and only communicating through a platform chat with no company identity.

Should I use a third-party inspection?

For any order of meaningful value, yes. Independent inspectors (SGS, TUV, AQSIQ-licensed local firms) confirm quantity, quality and packaging before you release the balance.

How should I structure payment safely?

Never prepay 100%. A common safe structure is T/T 30% deposit to start, 70% after QC pass and before shipment.

Is a trading company safer than a factory?

Not inherently. What matters is verifiable identity and a single accountable party. A coordinator that discloses its operating entity and vetted network is safer than an anonymous factory.

What is the fastest red flag in a supplier check?

A mismatch between the licence name, the bank account name and the invoice entity. That single inconsistency should stop a deposit until it is explained in writing.

Can I verify remotely without flying to China?

Yes. We provide a by-process photo gallery, a recorded shop-floor video, a certificate and document wall, and live inspection by appointment; a third-party inspection can be added before shipment.
