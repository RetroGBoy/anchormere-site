Reading a PCBA and SMT capability sheet: ten checks before you award the build

读懂 PCBA 与 SMT 能力表：下单前十项核查

A capability sheet is a marketing document until you test it against your own board. These are the ten questions that separate a factory able to build your PCBA reliably from one that can only quote it, with the standards bodies behind each check.

在拿你自己的板子验证之前，能力表只是营销材料。以下十个问题可区分「能稳定做出你的 PCBA」与「只会报价」的工厂，并附每项对应的标准机构出处。

The ten checks

01. Confirm layer count and board class确认层数与板材等级

Ask which layer counts, materials and copper weights the line runs in volume — not as a one-off — and whether the IPC class (1/2/3) is stated on the quote.

确认量产（而非偶尔打样）可做的层数、材料与铜厚，并看报价是否注明 IPC 等级（1/2/3 级）。

02. Read trace width, spacing and via rules核对线宽线距与过孔规则

Minimum trace/space, via diameter and annular ring define what your layout can safely use; these should be published limits, not verbal assurances.

最小线宽/线距、过孔孔径与环宽决定版图可用的安全边界；这些应是书面公布的限值，而非口头承诺。

03. Check the smallest package they place核对其可贴装的最小封装

Fine-pitch components, BGAs and 0201 or smaller passives require specific placement accuracy and X-ray capability — ask which lines have it.

细间距元件、BGA 与 0201 及更小被动件需要相应贴装精度与 X-Ray 能力——问清哪条线具备。

04. Ask for the reflow profile and control索取回流曲线与管控方式

A documented reflow profile with zone temperatures, conveyor speed and regular oven profiling is a baseline process-control signal.

带各温区温度、链速与定期炉温测试的书面 回流曲线，是最基本的工艺管控信号。

05. Require a DFM report before release投产前要求 DFM 报告

A DFM review should come back as written findings on pad geometry, clearance, fiducials and panelisation, not a simple OK.

DFM 评审应返回关于焊盘几何、间距、基准点与 拼板的书面问题清单，而非一句「可做」。

06. Specify inspection and test coverage明确检测与测试覆盖率

AOI catches placement and solder defects; ICT or functional test catches electrical faults. Ask what percentage of each build is tested and how failures are logged.

AOI 查贴装与焊接缺陷，ICT 或功能测试查电气故障；问清每批抽检比例与失效记录方式。

07. Control the fabrication data revision管控制造数据版本

Release Gerber or ODB++ plus drill and stack-up files with a revision number and checksum, and require written acknowledgement of the exact build revision.

Gerber/ODB++、钻孔与叠层文件需带版本号与校验值发布，并要求工厂书面确认具体生产版本。

08. Verify compliance for wired interfaces核验有线接口的合规

If the board exposes USB, the USB-IF specifications and compliance documents define connector, power and logo-use requirements you should design to.

若板卡引出 USB，USB-IF 规范与合规文件定义了连接器、供电及标识使用的设计要求。

09. Check measurement and calibration practice核查测量与校准体系

Test equipment should be traceable to national measurement standards; NIST's measurement science and IEC standards are the usual reference points.

测试设备应可溯源至国家计量标准；NIST 计量科学与 IEC 标准是通常的参照依据。

10. Tie the certificate to the specific product让证书对应具体产品

A UL certification or CB Scheme report is valid for the exact model and construction listed; confirm the file number and scope match your build.

UL 认证或 CB 体系报告只对所列型号与结构有效；核对档案号与覆盖范围是否与你的产品一致。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

PCBA

Printed Circuit Board Assembly: a bare PCB populated with components, as distinct from the bare board itself.

已贴装元器件的印制电路板组件，区别于裸板 PCB。

SMT

Surface-mount technology: components are placed directly onto pads and reflow-soldered, enabling high-density automated assembly.

表面贴装技术，元件直接贴于焊盘并回流焊接，适合高密度自动化组装。

BOM

Bill of materials: the complete part list with manufacturer names, part numbers and approved alternates.

完整物料清单，含厂商、料号与认可替代料。

DFM

Design for Manufacture: a factory review that flags pad, clearance and panelisation issues before the board is released to production.

可制造性设计评审，在投产前指出焊盘、间距与拼板问题。

AOI

Automated Optical Inspection: camera-based checking of solder joints and component placement after reflow.

自动光学检测，回流焊后以相机检查焊点与贴装位置。

ICT

In-Circuit Test: a bed-of-nails fixture that verifies component values, opens and shorts on a loaded board.

在线测试，通过针床夹具检查已贴装板的元件值、开路和短路。

Reflow profile

The controlled time-temperature curve in a reflow oven that determines solder joint quality and component stress.

回流炉内受控的时间-温度曲线，决定焊点质量与元件热应力。

Panelization

Arranging several boards into one production panel with rails and fiducials to improve SMT throughput.

将多块板拼成带工艺边与基准点的生产拼板，提升贴装效率。

Gerber / ODB++

The standard fabrication data set (copper layers, solder mask, drill files) released to a PCB house.

交付给板厂的制造数据（铜层、阻焊、钻孔等）标准格式。

First-pass yield

The share of units that pass all tests without rework on the first run; a direct indicator of process maturity.

一次通过全部测试、无需返修的比例，直接反映工艺成熟度。
