Three models, three risk profiles

What an “OEM marketplace” actually means

“OEM 交易平台”到底指什么

The same term covers three different businesses. They look alike in search results and behave very differently once money moves.

Listing or directory

Factories and traders publish products and capability claims. Discovery is cheap and instant; nothing on the page has been verified by anyone.

RFQ with escrow

You post a requirement, suppliers quote, the platform holds payment until you accept. Escrow protects the transaction, not the production line.

Managed sourcing

One accountable party owns the specification, supplier qualification, inspection and export documents. You pay for coordination rather than for a listing.

Verification checklist

Seven checks before you message a marketplace supplier

联系平台供应商前的七项核验

The platform puts quotes in front of you; it does not check them. If any of these seven fails, the price does not mean anything yet.

  • Match the licence scope to the process you need — moulding, SMT, stamping, assembly — not to the product name. Verification steps.
  • Ask for the production address and compare it with the address on the licence. A registered office is not a workshop.
  • Ask which line your order will run on, and who owns the tooling after the last shipment.
  • Buy a sample from the production-intent line, not a hand-built golden unit.
  • Check certificates by holder name, issue date and scope. ISO 9001 is a quality system, not a product-safety approval.
  • Write AQL, acceptance criteria and third-party inspection rights into the PO, not into an email.
  • Compare landed cost — tooling, packaging, testing, freight, duty — not the unit price on the listing.
When not to use it

When a marketplace is the wrong tool

什么时候平台是错的工具

Marketplaces are built to surface offers, not to qualify them. Four cases where the model fails:

  • Custom tooling or IP you cannot publish: the moment your drawing is on a platform, it is on every competitor’s screen.
  • Regulated categories — medical, wireless, EV charging, children’s products: approvals attach to the factory and the BOM, not to the listing.
  • Mixed-SKU orders that must leave as one shipment with one set of export documents.
  • When nobody on your side can read the drawing: a marketplace will still quote, and the quote will be wrong.

If that is your case, start from OEM vs ODM or the sourcing-agent model instead.

FAQ

常见问题

What is an OEM marketplace?

A platform where factories and trading companies list products or answer requests for quotation. The term covers three different models — a listing directory, an RFQ marketplace with escrow, and a managed sourcing service — and only the third takes responsibility for qualifying the supplier.

Which marketplace is best for OEM electronics?

There is no single best platform. Alibaba and Made-in-China have the widest supplier base, Global Sources skews toward audited exporters, and 1688 shows domestic pricing but is Chinese-language and expects local payment. Use them to build a long list, then verify off-platform.

Can I get custom tooling through a marketplace?

You can find suppliers who offer it, but tooling ownership, trial runs and first-article approval are contract terms, not listing features. Confirm who owns the mould, where it is stored, and what happens to it if you change supplier.

How do I tell a factory from a trading company on a marketplace?

Check the licence scope against the process, compare the production address with the registered address, ask for the equipment list, and book a live shop-floor walkthrough. A trader can produce a catalogue, but not a line.

Are marketplace prices lower than a sourcing partner's?

The quoted unit price usually is, because it excludes tooling, testing, packaging and the cost of a failed batch. Compare landed cost at the same AQL and acceptance standard, and the gap narrows or reverses.
