Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

IoT modules are a calibration and connectivity problem: sensor drift, antenna detuning inside the enclosure and field firmware updates are the real lifecycle costs.

Process & technical notes

  • Sensor calibration coefficients are written per unit at the test station, not as one shared firmware constant.
  • Antenna matching is re-tuned in the final enclosure with battery and cabling present; a bare-board VSWR reading is not representative.
  • LPWAN choice (NB-IoT, LoRaWAN, Wi-Fi HaLow) is fixed by the deployment region and the operator certification list.

Buyer checklist

  • State the deployment country, network operator and power budget at RFQ; module certification is region-locked.
  • Require a calibration certificate traceable to a reference instrument and a defined recalibration interval.
  • Ask for the OTA rollback and key-provisioning plan before mass production, not after field failures.

QC & inspection focus

  • Per-unit calibration verification against a reference, plus a 24 h burn-in drift check on sampled lots.
  • TRP/TIS or conducted RF check in the assembled enclosure; IP-rated units go through an air-leak test.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you provide FCC / CE certified modules?

Yes. Many Wi-Fi/BLE/LoRa modules are pre-certified; delta testing is supported.

What is the battery life of a wireless sensor node?

Typically 5–10 years on a lithium cell, depending on reporting interval.

Can you do custom firmware and OTA?

Yes. AT commands, embedded firmware, OTA and secure boot.

Which protocols do your gateways support?

Modbus, MQTT, OPC UA, JSON and HTTP for SCADA/cloud.

Can I use my own cloud endpoint and device certificates?

Yes. Provisioning can use your PKI and endpoint; we document the provisioning flow at handover and keep the credentials off the production line logs.

What is the MOQ for a calibrated sensor module?

500 pcs per item applies to production; calibration fixtures and coefficients are set up once and reused across subsequent lots.
