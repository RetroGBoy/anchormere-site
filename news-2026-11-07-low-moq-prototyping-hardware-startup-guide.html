Low-MOQ manufacturing: ten points from prototype to first production run

低 MOQ 制造：从样机到首批量产的十项要点

Startups rarely fail because a factory cannot build the product — they fail because the jump from five prototypes to five hundred units changes the process, the cost structure and the compliance burden. These ten points describe that transition and how to keep it under control.

硬件初创失败往往不是因为工厂做不出产品，而是因为从 5 台样机跳到 500 台时，工艺、成本结构与合规负担全都变了。以下十项描述这一跃迁及如何把它管住。

The ten checks

01. Understand what actually sets the MOQ弄清 MOQ 由什么决定

MOQ follows material lot sizes, minimum board panel counts and line changeover time; asking which item sets the floor often reveals a negotiable constraint.

MOQ 由物料起批量、拼板最小数与换线时间决定；问清是哪项卡住下限，常能发现可谈的空间。

02. Separate prototype, pilot and production builds把样机、试产与量产分开

EVT / DVT / PVT are distinct gates: engineering feasibility, design validation, then production readiness on the real line. Skipping one moves defects downstream.

EVT / DVT / PVT 是三道独立关口：工程可行性、设计验证、正式产线的量产准备；跳过任何一道都会把缺陷推到下游。

03. Use rapid tooling before hard tooling先软模后硬模

Rapid tooling lets you validate fit, finish and assembly with aluminium or soft-steel moulds before committing to hardened production tools.

快速模具用铝模或软钢模先验证配合、外观与装配，再投入硬化量产模具。

04. Budget NRE as a separate line把 NRE 单列预算

Non-recurring engineering covers fixtures, programming and test development; folding it into unit price hides the real cost of a small run.

一次性工程费覆盖夹具、编程与测试开发；并入单价会掩盖小批量的真实成本。

05. Apply DFMA before the drawing is released图纸发布前先做 DFMA

DFMA reduces part count and fastener variety, which matters more to low-volume cost than shaving a few cents off any single component.

DFMA 减少零件与紧固件种类，对低量成本的影响远大于在单个料上省几分钱。

06. Run a pilot on the actual line在正式产线上试产

A pilot run validates takt time, work instructions and first-pass yield in conditions that a hand-built prototype cannot reproduce.

试产在手工样机无法复现的条件下验证节拍、作业指导书与 一次通过良率。

07. Lock the BOM before scaling放量前锁定 BOM

A released BOM with named manufacturers and approved alternates prevents the silent substitutions that small batches often attract.

发布带厂商名与认可替代料的 BOM，可避免小批量最易出现的悄悄换料。

08. Plan wireless qualification early尽早规划无线认证

Products using Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or USB interfaces carry qualification and logo-use requirements that should shape the design, not follow it.

采用 Wi-Fi、蓝牙或 USB 接口的产品有认证与标识使用要求，应影响设计而非事后补救。

09. Protect the design before you share it分享设计前先做保护

File through the USPTO or WIPO route and use an NNN agreement before sending Gerbers and artwork to any prospective workshop.

在把 Gerber 与美术稿发给任何候选车间之前，先经 USPTO 或 WIPO 途径申请，并签署 NNN 协议。

10. Consolidate mixed SKUs to reach the floor用混 SKU 拼单跨过起订门槛

Where a single item cannot meet a factory floor, mixed-SKU consolidation across related parts can reach it without forcing you to overstock one variant.

单品达不到起订量时，用相关品类的混 SKU 拼单可以跨过门槛，而不必为单一型号压库存。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

MOQ

Minimum order quantity: the smallest volume a factory will run, driven by material lot sizes and line changeover cost.

工厂可承接的最小订单量，由物料批量与换线成本决定。

EVT / DVT / PVT

Engineering, Design and Production Verification Test stages — the three hardware build gates before mass production.

工程、设计、生产验证三个硬件试制关口，量产前依次通过。

Rapid tooling

Short-run aluminium or soft-steel moulds used for prototype and pilot batches before committing to hardened production tooling.

试模用铝模或软钢模，用于样机与试产，避免过早投入硬模。

NRE

Non-recurring engineering: one-off setup, fixture and programming charges billed separately from unit price.

一次性工程费：夹具、编程与调试等单独计费，不计入单价。

DFMA

Design for Manufacture and Assembly: simplifying part count and fasteners so a product can be built and servied reliably.

面向制造与装配的设计，减少零件与紧固件数量以提升可制造性。

Pilot run

A small pre-production batch on the actual line used to validate takt time, yield and work instructions.

在正式产线进行的小批试产，用于验证节拍、良率与作业指导书。

First-pass yield

The share of units that pass all tests without rework on the first run; a direct indicator of process maturity.

一次通过全部测试、无需返修的比例，直接反映工艺成熟度。

BOM

Bill of materials: the complete part list with manufacturer names, part numbers and approved alternates.

完整物料清单，含厂商、料号与认可替代料。

Golden sample

The sealed, signed reference unit that both parties use as the acceptance baseline for mass production.

双方封样签认的基准样机，作为量产验收依据。

PCBA

Printed Circuit Board Assembly: a bare PCB populated with components, as distinct from the bare board itself.

已贴装元器件的印制电路板组件，区别于裸板 PCB。
