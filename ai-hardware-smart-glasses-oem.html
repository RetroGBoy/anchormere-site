Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

AI-native devices are not an existing product with a feature bolted on. Smart glasses, AI translation pens, voice recorders and screen-less smart bands are designed from the chip, sensor and algorithm layer upward, and Greater Bay Area output of AI hardware grew sharply through 2026 as component markets in Huaqiangbei retooled around them. Anchormere integrates the physical build: optical and display modules, low-power PCBA, micro-acoustics, battery and charging, injection-moulded or metal enclosures, and final assembly and test. Circuit design and core algorithm R&D stay with specialist electronics partners; single-point accountability and the quality baseline always sit with us. That split matters for buyers, because the failure modes of an AI device are usually physical — hinge fatigue, microphone wind noise, battery swelling, optical alignment and thermal drift — not algorithmic. We quote to the drawing, run a pilot batch at 500 pcs, and hand your RFQ a capability sheet that says exactly which parts we own and which your silicon or model partner owns.

Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

AI-native hardware fails at the mechanical and acoustical edges long before it fails at the model layer: hinge fatigue, optical alignment drift, microphone wind noise and heat in a sealed enclosure.

Process & technical notes

  • Optical modules are aligned on a fixed fixture and the alignment is re-checked after thermal cycling, not only at first assembly.
  • MEMS microphone arrays are tuned with wind-noise and echo tests in the assembled enclosure, since gasket and cavity shape change the response.
  • Battery and charging contacts go through cycle and temperature checks before a pilot batch is released.

Buyer checklist

  • State clearly who owns the silicon, the model and the app; our scope is the physical build and test.
  • Provide the optical and acoustic acceptance criteria with the drawing, not after the first pilot run.
  • Ask for a written capability boundary so no one assumes the integrator also owns algorithm R&D.

QC & inspection focus

  • Hinge cycle and drop tests are run on pilot units with results filed against the golden sample.
  • A full-function check covers mic, speaker, display, wireless, charging and thermal behaviour on every pilot unit.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you design the chip or the AI model?

No. Circuit design and core algorithm R&D stay with specialist electronics partners. We own the physical integration, assembly, test and single-point accountability, and we state that boundary in writing at RFQ.

What is the MOQ for a new AI device?

500 pcs for a pilot run on an existing platform. New tooling or a custom optical module needs a longer sample phase, quoted separately.

Which parts of a smart glasses build do you handle?

Optical/display module sourcing and alignment, low-power PCBA, micro-acoustics, battery and charging case, enclosure, hinge and final assembly and test.

How do you validate wear-comfort and durability?

Hinge cycle tests, drop tests, thermal drift checks, microphone wind-noise tuning and battery cycle checks, all against an approved golden sample before the production slot.

Can you source pre-certified radio modules?

Where a Wi-Fi / BLE module is already certified, we source that module and keep its certificate with the document pack; delta testing is coordinated with your compliance partner. We do not claim certifications we have not verified.

Can you build the whole AI device including the algorithm?

We integrate the physical device and own the quality baseline, but core algorithm and chip design belong with specialist partners. We document the split at RFQ so responsibilities are unambiguous.

Why is a pilot run needed for smart glasses?

Optical alignment, hinge feel and acoustic behaviour only stabilise after a real assembly run. A 500-pc pilot exposes those variations before you commit to volume tooling.
