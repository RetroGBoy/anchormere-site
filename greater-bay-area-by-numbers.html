The Greater Bay Area by the numbers: why it is a sourcing powerhouse

大湾区数据一览：为何它是采购高地

The Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area (GBA) is a city cluster of eleven cities covering about 56,000 km². In 2024 it accounted for roughly one-ninth of China's economy from under 0.6% of its land area. For buyers, those headline numbers translate into dense, specialized supply chains within a single day's logistics.

Key 2024 figures

Population~87.5 million
GDP~¥14.8 trillion (≈ US$2.0 trillion) — comparable to a top global bay area
Goods trade~¥17.6 trillion in total imports & exports
CitiesGuangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Foshan, Huizhou, Dongguan, Zhongshan, Jiangmen, Zhaoqing + Hong Kong + Macao
InnovationShenzhen–Hong Kong–Guangzhou science cluster ranks among the world's top; ~9 trillion-level industrial clusters

Why it matters for sourcing

  • Density = speed. Components, tooling, finishing and packaging for a single product often sit within a 1–2 hour drive, which is why prototyping and small-batch runs move fast here.
  • Specialized clusters. Electronics & communications, metal & sheet fabrication, home appliances, lighting, new energy and industrial automation each concentrate in specific GBA cities.
  • Open, integrated logistics. World-class ports and airports, plus cross-boundary links such as the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge and Shenzhen–Zhongshan Channel, keep goods moving across the cluster.
  • Depth of suppliers. Tens of thousands of manufacturers — from large OEMs to tiny mixed-SKU workshops — give buyers both scale and flexibility.
