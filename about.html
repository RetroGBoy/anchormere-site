About Anchormere

We are the China sourcing office you wish you had hired years ago.

你一直想雇的那支中国采购团队

Anchormere is your China sourcing partner for the Greater Bay Area, operated by 深圳森亚实业有限公司. We turn the region's fragmented manufacturing clusters into one accountable delivery — sourcing, build-to-print (OEM) and joint manufacturing for overseas buyers. We lead today in AI data-center hardware and extend to adjacent clusters.

Why "Anchor"

为什么叫「锚泊」

In supply-chain language, an anchor supplier is the one you trust when conditions get rough. That is the role we are built for: stable, accountable, and present when things need fixing.

We are not a factory

We are a coordinator. That means we are not loyal to one workshop's capacity — we route your order to the right one, and we own the outcome either way.

We are not a marketplace

A marketplace shows you 200 suppliers and wishes you luck. We show you one accountable partner — us — and we answer when something goes wrong.

What we actually do

我们实际做什么

Sourcing & procurement

Find, qualify and procure across Greater Bay Area clusters for your category — one accountable buyer, not twenty suppliers.

OEM / build-to-print

Turn your drawing or sample into a production run, routed to the right workshop, tooling cost carried by the buyer where applicable.

Joint manufacturing

Co-develop, share tooling and scale with you across the base — from first article to volume.

The team

Two decades on the factory floor

报价背后的团队：二十年制造，十年开发，十年运营

Anchormere did not begin as a website — it began on the shop floor. Our core team has spent more than 20 years inside Greater Bay Area manufacturing: the kind of time that teaches you which workshops cut corners and which ones earn the repeat orders. A good part of that was spent turning a customer's drawing or sample into a product that actually ships — 10+ years of product development. And alongside it, 10+ years of marketing and operations: the commercial instinct and export discipline that get goods onto a buyer's dock, on time, with the right paperwork.

We built Anchormere so an overseas buyer can borrow that institutional memory — the judgement of a local sourcing office — without staffing one. When you message us, the person who replies has very likely seen your part made a hundred times before.

Behind that experience sits a complete, end-to-end team — R&D, mass production and delivery — not a one-person desk. That is the structure which has let us turn a brief into a category best-seller more than once. We are sincerely looking for long-term partners: bring the market, we bring the supply chain, and we grow the business together.

我们拥有完整的研发、量产、交付团队，有打造爆款的经验，现诚挚寻找合作伙伴，携手共赢、再创佳绩。
