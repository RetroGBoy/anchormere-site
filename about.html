About Anchormere

We are the China sourcing office you wish you had hired years ago.

你一直想雇的那支中国采购团队

Anchormere is your China sourcing partner for the Greater Bay Area, operated by 深圳森亚实业有限公司. We turn the region's fragmented manufacturing clusters into one accountable delivery — sourcing, build-to-print (OEM) and joint manufacturing for overseas buyers. We lead today in AI data-center hardware and extend to adjacent clusters.

Why "Anchor"

为什么叫「锚泊」

In supply-chain language, an anchor supplier is the one you trust when conditions get rough. That is the role we are built for: stable, accountable, and present when things need fixing.

We are not a factory

We are a coordinator. That means we are not loyal to one workshop's capacity — we route your order to the right one, and we own the outcome either way.

We are not a marketplace

A marketplace shows you 200 suppliers and wishes you luck. We show you one accountable partner — us — and we answer when something goes wrong.

What we actually do

我们实际做什么

Sourcing & procurement

Find, qualify and procure across Greater Bay Area clusters for your category — one accountable buyer, not twenty suppliers.

OEM / build-to-print

Turn your drawing or sample into a production run, routed to the right workshop, tooling cost carried by the buyer where applicable.

Joint manufacturing

Co-develop, share tooling and scale with you across the base — from first article to volume.
