How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Finding a factory is a verification sequence: licence, capability match, sample, audit, then a contract with an inspection clause — never a price comparison first.

What we do, step by step

  • Check the business licence, registered capital, legal representative and whether the entity actually holds the claimed factory.
  • Match the capability to your category: an injection shop is not a PCBA house, and a trading company is not a factory.
  • Order a paid sample from the production-intent line, then audit before any deposit.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Request a by-process photo gallery, a recorded shop-floor video and a live inspection by appointment.
  • Verify certificates by holder name and issue date, and check the scope covers your product.
  • Write acceptance criteria, AQL level and third-party inspection rights into the contract.

How we verify & report

  • Cross-check the invoice entity, the bank account name and the licence name — mismatches are the clearest red flag.
  • Confirm the production address matches the audit address, not only the registered office.

From written specification to normalised RFQ

从书面规格到拉齐的询价表

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • Start from a written specification, not from a supplier list: a drawing, target quantity, destination market, packaging spec and acceptance standard are what make two quotations comparable.
  • Verification has layers — business licence scope, export record, equipment list, quality-system certificate validity and bank account name — and a catalogue or a trade-show booth establishes none of them.
  • Sample evaluation should be distinct from supplier evaluation: a good hand-made sample proves the factory can build one unit, not that it can hold tolerance across 5,000.
  • Use a normalised RFQ sheet with the same Incoterm, AQL and packaging on every line; otherwise a low quote usually means a missing cost, not a more efficient factory.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer shortlists five factories from a marketplace and asks which is best. We normally rebuild one RFQ sheet and send it to all five, then compare the returned quotations plus a verification record; the ranking often changes once the specs are identical.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

How do I verify a Chinese manufacturer?

Use a by-process photo gallery, a recorded shop-floor video, a certificate wall and a live inspection by appointment — no blind trust.

What is a factory audit checklist?

Licenses, production capacity, QC process, certifications and verifiable references. We provide ours on request.

How do I avoid scams when manufacturing in China?

Verify the legal entity, use staged payments (30% deposit / 70% before shipment), request NNN, and inspect before full payment.

What does MOQ mean in manufacturing?

Minimum Order Quantity — the smallest batch a factory accepts. Ours starts at 500 pcs per SKU with consolidation.

How do I tell a factory from a trading company?

Ask for the licence scope, the production address, equipment list and a live shop-floor walkthrough; an integrator like Anchormere should state plainly that it coordinates vetted workshops rather than owning one factory.

Should I always choose the lowest quote?

No. Compare quotes only after scope, BOM, AQL, tooling ownership and payment terms are identical; an unusually low number usually hides a material substitution or an excluded test.
