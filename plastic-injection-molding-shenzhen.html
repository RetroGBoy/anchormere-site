Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Injection moulding is decided at the tool design review: gate position, draft angle and wall thickness fix both cosmetic sink marks and the per-unit cycle time.

Process & technical notes

  • Mould-flow review sets gate location and weld-line position before steel is cut; weld lines on a visible face cannot be polished away.
  • Uniform wall thickness with proper ribs prevents sink and warp; a 0.5 mm local thickening can add seconds to the cooling cycle.
  • T1 samples are measured against the drawing, T2 after tool correction, then the tool is signed off with a dimension report.

Buyer checklist

  • Provide 3D data plus critical-to-quality dimensions and the resin grade; material choice drives shrink rate and tool steel.
  • Agree who owns the tool and where it is stored; tool ownership must be explicit before the deposit.
  • State surface finish (VDI / SPI / texture) and colour standard up front, since texture changes require re-polishing the tool.

QC & inspection focus

  • First-article dimension report on T1/T2 with Cpk on critical dimensions; shot-weight consistency logged per lot.
  • Cosmetic inspection under agreed lighting for sink, flash, weld lines and colour delta against the approved master.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

I have no 3D drawing, how do I start?

Send a sample or sketch; we finish the 3D drawing and confirm before tooling (T1 sample).

What is the tooling lead time?

15–25 days for a standard mold; rush options are available.

Do you provide finishing like painting/plating?

Yes. Painting, printing, plating, anodizing and assembly.

What is the MOQ for molded parts?

500 pcs per SKU; trial runs are supported.

What does the 500 pcs MOQ mean for a custom moulded part?

The mould is a one-time tooling cost; production runs start at 500 pcs per item, and mixed-SKU consolidation lets several moulded parts share one shipment.

Can you work from a sample part without 3D drawings?

Yes for reverse-engineering cases: we 3D-scan the sample, review the reconstructed model with you, and only cut steel after you approve the dimensions.
