Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

SMT yield is engineered at the stencil and reflow profile, then proven by SPI, AOI and X-ray — not by a final functional test alone.

Process & technical notes

  • Stencil aperture design and nano-coating are set by pad geometry; insufficient paste on fine-pitch QFN is the top defect driver.
  • A board-specific reflow profile is recorded and locked; oven drift is caught by a weekly thermal profiler run.
  • Conformal coating, if required, is masked at connectors and test points before the board enters the coating line.

Buyer checklist

  • Provide Gerber, BOM, pick-and-place file and a test spec together; a missing CPL file is the most common RFQ delay.
  • State turnkey or consigned, and the accepted alternate-part policy, in the RFQ.
  • Agree first-pass yield target and the rework rule (how many touch-ups allowed per board) before the pilot.

QC & inspection focus

  • SPI on 100% of prints, AOI after reflow and X-ray on all BGA/QFN/bottom-terminated parts.
  • ICT/FCT with a logged serial number per board; defect Pareto issued with each lot.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can you start with a single prototype board?

Yes. Many Shenzhen shops support 1-piece SMT prototypes with 24–48h turnaround.

Do you do turnkey (we supply BOM or you source)?

Both. Consignment (you supply parts) or turnkey (we source + assemble).

How do you ensure quality?

AOI, X-ray (BGA), ICT and functional test with 100% inspection options.

What files do you need to quote?

Gerber, BOM, centroid/placement file and assembly drawing; quote within 48h.

Do you accept consigned components?

Yes. Consigned parts are stored by lot code, moisture-sensitive devices are baked and tracked, and unused stock is reconciled at shipment.

Is a pilot run possible below 500 pcs?

Yes. Prototype and pilot builds can start from a handful of boards; the 500 pcs per item MOQ applies to production runs, not to engineering validation.
