Certifications & Compliance

Certifications we work to — and the documents you get.

支持的认证体系与随货单证说明

We work to internationally recognized standards and arrange certification through accredited bodies. Certificates are held by the manufacturing workshop and issued per purchase order, as your market requires. This page lists what we can support — ask before quoting and we confirm in writing.

Clarification / 澄清声明： The certifications and standards listed on this page are held and issued by our partner factories and accredited third-party bodies — not by Anchormere itself. Anchormere is a sourcing integrator; we help you obtain the relevant compliance documents and test reports before quotation and order placement.

上述认证与标准由合作工厂及具备资质的第三方机构持有与出具，并非 Anchormere 自身认证。作为采购整合方，我们协助您在报价与下单前获取相应合规文件与检测报告。

Management system standards

管理体系认证（工厂持有）

ISO 9001

Quality management systems. Held by our vetted workshops; certificate copies shared per PO on request.

ISO 14001

Environmental management systems — relevant for EU buyers and ESG reporting.

IATF 16949

Automotive quality standard, available through partner workshops on request for automotive orders.

Product & market-entry certifications

产品与市场准入认证（按市场安排）

CE marking EU

Conformity for EU market entry — LVD, EMC and RoHS directives as applicable. Declaration of DoC plus test reports per product.

UKCA UK

UK market conformity, arranged in parallel with CE where scopes align.

FCC US

FCC compliance for electronics with radio or clocked circuits, via accredited US-listed labs.

RoHS & REACH

Substance-compliance test reports (SGS / TÜV / Intertek) for electronics and metal parts shipped to the EU.

IPC-A-610

Electronics assembly workmanship standard applied to PCBA and box-build orders — Class 2 default, Class 3 on request.

UL On request

UL listing / recognition arranged through accredited labs where your product category requires it — confirm scope before quoting.

Third-party inspection & audit

第三方验货与审核

Per-shipment inspection

SGS / TÜV / Intertek-style inspection reports arranged on request — AQL sampling, photos and dimensional records.

Factory audit

Third-party factory audits (capability, QMS, social compliance) coordinated before first order.

Material & test certs

Material certificates (mill certs), salt-spray, tensile and plating-thickness reports per spec, per batch.
