Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Smart-home modules are a protocol and mains-safety problem first: Matter / Wi-Fi / Zigbee choice and creepage distances shape the whole enclosure.

Process & technical notes

  • Radio coexistence between Wi-Fi, BLE and Zigbee/Thread is validated in-situ, since relay switching noise raises the packet-error floor.
  • Mains-fed relay or dimmer layouts keep creepage and clearance to the low-voltage antenna area; the PCB cut-out is a safety feature, not a cost cut.
  • OTA firmware and device credentials are provisioned at the test station so each unit ships with a unique identity.

Buyer checklist

  • Name the target ecosystem (Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, Matter) before RFQ; certification and app review lead times differ.
  • Specify 100–240 V or a fixed regional voltage, plus plug standard, at the enquiry stage.
  • Require a documented OTA rollback plan — a failed field update is the most common warranty claim.

QC & inspection focus

  • Hi-pot / dielectric withstand test on every mains-fed unit, plus relay endurance cycling on sampled lots.
  • Radio TX power and packet-error-rate check in the final assembled enclosure, not on a bare board.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you support Matter / Thread / Zigbee?

Yes. We source and integrate Wi-Fi, BLE, Zigbee, Thread and Matter-ready modules.

Can you build a custom IoT sensor node?

Yes. We integrate MCU, wireless module, battery and enclosure with low-power firmware and OTA.

What certifications apply to smart-home RF modules?

FCC, CE-RED and RoHS; pre-certified modules are available.

Do you provide the app and cloud bridging?

Yes. Firmware, app and cloud/API bridging are part of our integration scope.

Can you build to a Matter-certified reference design?

Yes. We work from a certified module and keep the radio section unchanged so the existing certification can be transferred rather than re-earned.

Who handles cloud and app integration?

We integrate and test the hardware and provisioning flow; cloud backend and consumer app remain with your team or your platform partner, with the interface documented at handover.
