Cross-border e-commerce: sourcing from Guangdong for global storefronts

跨境电商：从广东为海外店铺采购

China's cross-border e-commerce keeps expanding. In 2024 the country's cross-border online trade reached roughly ¥2.6–2.7 trillion, growing double digits, with exports — led by consumer goods and electronics — the dominant share. Guangdong is consistently among the top sourcing provinces. For online sellers, that means unmatched access to small-batch supply.

Key 2024 figures

Cross-border trade~¥2.6–2.7 trillion (+~11% to +14% yoy)
Exports~¥2.15 trillion (+~17% yoy); consumer goods ~97% of export value
Top destinationsUnited States, United Kingdom, Germany
Top sourcing regionsGuangdong, Zhejiang, Fujian, Jiangsu, Shandong

What online sellers should optimize

  • Low MOQ & fast restock — Guangdong's dense supplier base supports 500-piece runs and quick reorders.
  • Mixed-SKU consolidation — combine several products into one shipment to cut freight.
  • Quality at speed — sample verification and inline QC prevent returns that kill marketplace ratings.
  • Compliant docs — proper declarations and certificates keep parcels moving through customs.
