Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Smart toys sit at the intersection of toy safety and electronics compliance — EN71 / ASTM F963 and radio plus battery rules apply to the very same unit.

Process & technical notes

  • Voice, screen and plush-electronic hybrids are integrated on one PCBA; placement drives both EMC behaviour and small-parts safety.
  • Firmware is locked before safety testing — a late voice-library change can force a full retest of the radio module.
  • Battery compartments use captive screws and tool-access-only covers to satisfy accessible-battery clauses.

Buyer checklist

  • Fix the age grading first: 0–3, 3–8 and 8+ trigger different small-parts, torque and chemical migration limits.
  • Request EN71-1/2/3, ASTM F963, CPSIA plus CE RED / FCC and UN38.3 as one test plan, not separate orders.
  • Confirm who owns the voice content and firmware source before tooling; content licensing is separate from hardware.

QC & inspection focus

  • Small-parts torque and tension tests after drop and impact conditioning, not only on fresh samples.
  • Saliva and sweat simulant wipe tests on printed decals and painted surfaces; check for phthalate and heavy-metal migration.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do your toys meet EN71 / ASTM F963?

Yes. We support EN71, ASTM F963, CPC and CE; test reports and documentation are provided per target market.

Can you build a toy from my prototype or idea?

Yes. We handle industrial design, electronics, firmware and plush+electronic assembly from a sample, drawing or concept.

What is the prototype lead time?

Typically 10–15 days for a functional prototype.

Is there a minimum MOQ for custom toys?

500 pcs per SKU; trial orders are supported.

Can one SKU be certified for both the EU and US?

Yes, but the test plan must be written for both upfront; retesting after a failed US children's-product clause is far more expensive than a dual-market plan.

Do you handle plush plus electronics in one shipment?

Yes. Plush shells, PCBA and packaging are consolidated into one export document package with the toy and electronics certificates kept together.
