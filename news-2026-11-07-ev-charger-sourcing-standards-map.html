EV charger sourcing: ten standards points buyers should specify

充电桩采购：买家应写进规格书的十项标准要点

Charging hardware is a standards-defined product: connector geometry, control pilot behaviour and enclosure protection are all written down before a single tool is cut. These ten points map the standards to the decisions a buyer must make when sourcing from the Greater Bay Area.

充电硬件是标准定义出来的产品：接口几何、控制导引行为与外壳防护在开模之前都已写死。以下十项把标准对应到买家在大湾区采购时必须做出的决策。

The ten checks

01. Fix the connector family per market按市场锁定接口体系

CCS dominates European and North American deployments via CharIN, while CHAdeMO persists in installed fleets; China uses its own GB/T family.

欧美以 CharIN 推动的 CCS 为主，CHAdeMO 在存量车队中仍在用，中国采用 GB/T 体系。

02. Cite IEC 62196 for the interface接口引用 IEC 62196

IEC 62196 covers plugs, socket-outlets, vehicle connectors and inlets for conductive charging — the starting point for any connector drawing.

IEC 62196 覆盖传导充电的插头、插座、车辆连接器与车辆接口，是任何接口图纸的起点。

03. Cite IEC 61851 for the system系统引用 IEC 61851

IEC 61851 defines the charging system, control pilot and safety requirements; Mode 3 is the usual AC reference for dedicated EVSE.

IEC 61851 定义充电系统、控制导引与安全要求；专用交流供电设备通常参照 模式 3。

04. Specify the enclosure protection class明确外壳防护等级

An IP rating under IEC 60529 (for example IP54 for indoor units, IP67 for outdoor) should be written into the drawing and verified by test report.

按 IEC 60529 的 IP 等级（室内如 IP54、户外如 IP67）应写入图纸并以测试报告验证。

05. Confirm the backend protocol确认后台通信协议

OCPP compatibility determines whether a charge point can join an operator's management system — a firmware decision with a hardware cost.

OCPP 兼容性决定充电桩能否接入运营商管理平台——这是固件决策，却带来硬件成本。

06. Check cable and thermal design核查线缆与热设计

Cable cross-section, temperature rise and continuous-current rating should be validated at the maximum rated output, not at nominal conditions.

线缆截面积、温升与持续载流能力应按最大额定输出验证，而非标称工况。

07. Plan certification by destination按目的市场规划认证

UL certification and the IECEE CB Scheme are common routes for power-electronics evidence; the IEC standards catalogue names the test editions.

UL 认证与 IECEE CB 体系是电力电子证据的常见路径；IEC 标准目录给出测试标准版本。

08. Document efficiency and standby behaviour记录能效与待机表现

ENERGY STAR and DOE resources are useful benchmarks for efficiency expectations on charging and power-conversion equipment.

能源之星与 美国能源部资料可作为充电与功率转换设备能效预期的参照。

09. Audit component traceability审核元器件可追溯性

Batch traceability on connectors, contactors and cable assemblies is what makes a field recall manageable rather than existential.

连接器、接触器与线缆组件的 批次可追溯，决定现场召回是可控事件还是致命事件。

10. Consolidate the BOM across workshops跨车间整合 BOM

Charging assemblies span moulding, sheet metal, PCBA and cable harness; consolidating them into one inspection and one export pack is where integration earns its keep.

充电总成横跨注塑、钣金、PCBA 与线束；把它们整合为一次检验、一套出口单证，正是整合方的价值所在。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

CCS

Combined Charging System: the AC/DC connector family promoted by CharIN for European and North American EVs.

CharIN 推动的欧美电动车交直流一体充电接口体系。

CHAdeMO

A DC fast-charging connector and protocol standard originating in Japan, still present in installed fleets.

源自日本的直流快充接口与协议标准，存量车队仍在用。

IEC 62196

The IEC series covering EV plugs, socket-outlets, vehicle connectors and vehicle inlets for conductive charging.

IEC 关于电动汽车传导充电插头、插座与车辆接口的标准系列。

IEC 61851

The IEC series defining EV conductive charging systems, control pilot functions and safety requirements.

IEC 关于电动汽车传导充电系统、控制导引与安全要求的标准系列。

Mode 3

The IEC 61851 charging mode using a dedicated EVSE with a control pilot function for AC charging.

IEC 61851 定义的交流充电模式，使用带控制导引的专用供电设备。

OCPP

Open Charge Point Protocol: an open communication protocol between charge points and a central management system.

充电桩与后台管理系统之间的开放通信协议。

IP rating

An IEC 60529 ingress-protection code (e.g. IP54, IP67) describing dust and water resistance of an enclosure.

IEC 60529 外壳防护等级代码，描述防尘与防水能力。

PDU

Power Distribution Unit: the rack-mounted unit that delivers and, in metered variants, monitors power to installed equipment.

机柜内配电单元，计量型还可监测各回路用电。

Busbar

A rigid conductive bar used to distribute high current within a rack or power distribution assembly.

机柜或配电组件内用于分配大电流的刚性导电排。

Batch traceability

The ability to link a finished unit back to its material lots, machine, operator and test record via a lot or serial code.

通过批次或序列号追溯到物料批、机台、操作员与测试记录的能力。
