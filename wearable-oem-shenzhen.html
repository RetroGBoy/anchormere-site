Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Wearables fail in the field on sealing and sensor accuracy, not on the PCB — IP rating and optical calibration are the two gates that matter.

Process & technical notes

  • Optical PPG and accelerometer placement is fixed against the enclosure curvature; a 0.5 mm shift changes heart-rate signal quality.
  • IP67/IP68 sealing is achieved with gasket compression plus ultrasonic welding or adhesive bonding, validated by air-leak test.
  • Strap material, quick-release lug and skin-contact coatings are qualified together for nickel release and sweat corrosion.

Buyer checklist

  • Fix the sensor feature set (SpO2, ECG, GPS) at RFQ — each addition changes the BOM, power budget and regulatory class.
  • Ask for a water-bath and thermal-cycling report on production units, not just a prototype IP certificate.
  • Confirm charger pogo-pin plating and magnet polarity if you plan a shared dock across SKUs.

QC & inspection focus

  • 100% air-leak test after final assembly; sample units go through 1 m immersion and 48 h humidity soak.
  • Optical sensor calibration against a reference device on a sampled basis, with drift logged after burn-in.

FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Is the smartwatch compatible with Android and iOS?

Yes. Our firmware pairs with both via a companion app; we can also supply the app.

Can I put my own brand on the watch?

Yes. Custom logo, strap, watch face UI and packaging under private label; MOQ 500 pcs.

What sensors can be integrated?

Heart-rate, SpO2, accelerometer, GPS and body-temperature options.

Do you provide OTA firmware updates?

Yes, OTA update and pairing are supported.

Can I reuse an existing enclosure design for my brand?

Yes for ODM-derived tooling, provided the sealing geometry is re-validated; we re-run the air-leak and drop tests rather than relying on the original certificate.

What does the 500 pcs MOQ cover for wearables?

500 pcs per SKU covers one colourway and one firmware build; mixed-SKU consolidation across strap colours is allowed within a single shipment.
