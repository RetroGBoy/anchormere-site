Why source the chain, not just a board

为什么要按整条链来采购

A printed circuit board is not one purchase. It is a chain: copper foil, glass cloth and resin become copper-clad laminate; laminate is pressed, drilled and plated into a bare board; the bare board is populated, tested and integrated into a rack. AI compute hardware has tightened every link at once — layer counts, low-loss materials, substrate area and thermal design now move together. This board maps that chain to the pages where we document the engineering and sourcing decisions for each link, so a buyer can start at the material, the fabrication step or the finished assembly and still reach the same accountable quote.

AI-compute PCB trend terms buyers are searching in 2026

2026 AI 算力硬件的 PCB 热门趋势词

The terms below come from public industry reporting and describe what buyers currently search for. Anchormere does not claim any of these process capabilities, and they are neither a quotation nor a commitment.

Trend termWhy it matters now (public reporting)
AI server PCB, 20–30 layersMainstream AI computing boards moved from 8–16 layers to 20–30; ultra-high-layer backplanes for next-gen accelerator platforms are reported at 40 layers and above.
主流 AI 计算板由 8–16 层提升至 20–30 层；下一代加速平台的超高多层背板公开报道达 40 层以上。
M8 / M9 low-loss CCLLow-loss laminate grades are migrating from M7 toward M8/M9, paired with HVLP copper foil and low-Dk / Q-glass reinforcement for 224G-class SerDes and PCIe 6.0 channels.
低损耗板材等级由 M7 向 M8/M9 迁移，并搭配 HVLP 超低粗糙度铜箔与低介电/石英玻纤布，服务 224G 级 SerDes 与 PCIe 6.0 通道。
ABF / FC-BGA substrateLarger AI GPU and ASIC packages drive ABF build-up substrate area, layer count and fine line/space up; high-end ABF capacity is widely reported as the tightest link in the chain.
AI GPU 与 ASIC 封装面积增大，推高 ABF 增层载板的面积、层数与细线宽/线距；高端 ABF 产能被普遍报道为全链最紧环节。
HDI / mSAP / AnylayerModified semi-additive processing and any-layer microvia structures are now standard for high-density compute and optical-module boards, with line/space tightening to a few micrometres.
改良型半加成法（mSAP）与任意层微孔结构已成为高密度计算板与光模块板的主流工艺，线宽/线距收紧至数微米级。
800G / 1.6T optical module PCBOptical module boards climb from 10–12 layers at 800G to 12–16 at 1.6T, with near-package and co-packaged optics adding embedded capacitors and tighter insertion-loss budgets.
光模块板由 800G 的 10–12 层升至 1.6T 的 12–16 层；近封装/共封装光学进一步引入埋入电容与更严格的插损预算。
Liquid cooling & power deliveryCold plates, quick disconnects, CDUs and 48V/800V power shelves turn thermal and power hardware into a PCB-adjacent sourcing category for AI racks.
冷板、快接头、CDU 与 48V/800V 电源架，使热管理与供电硬件成为 AI 机柜里与 PCB 紧邻的采购品类。

FAQ

常见问题

Can you source a single link of the chain, or only a full board?

Both. We routinely source laminate and materials for a buyer's own fabricator, quote bare-board fabrication, or manage turnkey PCBA and system integration. The engagement boundary is stated in writing at RFQ.

Do you hold IPC, UL or other certifications for these boards?

No. IPC, IEC, JEDEC, IEEE, PCI-SIG and UL designations on these pages are public editorial references so buyers can ask a supplier for the matching evidence. Anchormere claims no certification and does not represent the issuing bodies.

Are the layer counts and material grades on this board guaranteed?

No. They are typical industry ranges drawn from public 2026 reporting, included so buyers know what to ask about. Binding values are those confirmed against your stack-up, drawing, target market and written RFQ.

What do you need to quote an AI-server or high-layer-count board?

A stack-up or Gerber/ODB++ set, BOM with manufacturer part numbers, impedance and insertion-loss requirements, target IPC class, test coverage by net, and the required documentation for the destination market.
