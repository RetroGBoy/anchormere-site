Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A laminate grade is not a single number. Tg, Td, CTE, Dk/Df at a stated frequency and copper roughness form a matched set; fixing only Tg leaves the rest open to substitution.

Process & technical notes

  • Prepreg resin content and flow are set by the press cycle and the copper distribution of the layer; quoting a resin content without a flow figure is not enough to reproduce the stack-up.
  • Copper foil type changes the loss budget: HTE, RTF and HVLP differ in surface roughness, which drives conductor loss at high frequency even when Dk/Df are unchanged.
  • T288 and Td describe thermal endurance of the resin system, not the assembly soldering limit; they should be requested as separate line items on the mill COA.

Buyer checklist

  • State Dk and Df together with the test method and frequency; a Df value without a frequency is not comparable between two mills.
  • Name the IPC-4101 slash sheet, copper weight and foil type in the PO so the quote and the delivered lot refer to the same material.
  • Ask whether the lot COA is per production batch or a generic type-test report, and require the batch number on the material packing list.

QC & inspection focus

  • Check peel strength, T288 delamination time and dimensional stability on the incoming lot, not only on a qualification sample.
  • For CAF-sensitive designs, ask for the CAF test coupon result and the glass-cloth silane treatment record for that lot.

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • The same FR-4 designation covers a wide Tg and Dk range; a PO that names only FR-4 allows the mill to substitute a resin system with a different press window and CTE.
  • Dk and Df are frequency-dependent; a datasheet value measured at 1 MHz cannot be used to predict a multi-gigabit channel, so the test method belongs in the RFQ.
  • Prepreg resin content and flow are a matched pair with the press cycle; changing copper distribution on an inner layer without re-checking flow produces resin starvation at the weave knuckles.
  • A generic type-test report is not a lot COA. Traceability requires the batch number on the packing list to match the tested lot, especially for CAF-sensitive designs.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer asks for a high-Tg laminate but does not state the Dk test frequency. Two mills quote the same Tg with different resin systems; the assembled boards then miss the impedance target because the pressed Dk differs. We normally hold the quote until the frequency, foil type and IPC-4101 slash sheet are all fixed, so the impedance model and the material baseline refer to the same datasheet.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

How can I verify the factory remotely?

What are the payment terms?

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What is a slash sheet and why does it matter?

It is the IPC-4101 specification sheet identifying a laminate's resin system, reinforcement and Tg. Quoting a grade without a slash sheet leaves the material ambiguous.

Do you hold UL or IPC certification for the laminate?

No. UL 94 and IPC references are public editorial references; the mill's own evidence is requested and passed through to the buyer for verification.

Can you source prepreg and core from different mills in one stack-up?

Hybrid sourcing is possible but must be declared. Mixing resin systems changes the press cycle and CTE, so it is agreed at RFQ rather than substituted later.

What files do you need to quote a laminate?

A stack-up, target Tg / Td / Dk / Df with test frequency, copper weight and foil type, plus the required IPC-4101 slash sheet and lot traceability depth.

Is high-Tg always better?

No. Higher Tg often means a more brittle resin system and a narrower press window. It should be selected against the assembly thermal profile, not by grade alone.

Can you supply a single lot for a multi-SKU order?

Yes. Material lots are consolidated and labelled per SKU so a buyer assembling several board types can keep one traceable material baseline.
