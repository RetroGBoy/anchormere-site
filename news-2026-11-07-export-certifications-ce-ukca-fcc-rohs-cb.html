Export certifications decoded: ten points on CE, UKCA, FCC, RoHS and CB

出口认证拆解：CE、UKCA、FCC、RoHS 与 CB 的十项要点

Certification is where good hardware gets stuck at the border. Buyers frequently ask a factory for the CE certificate — a document that, strictly, does not exist for most products. These ten points describe what each regime actually requires and which evidence you should hold.

认证是优质硬件最常卡在口岸的环节。买家常向工厂索要「CE 证书」，而对多数产品而言这份文件严格来说并不存在。以下十项说明各体系究竟要求什么、你该持有哪类证据。

The ten checks

01. Understand that CE is a declaration理解 CE 是自我声明

CE marking is the manufacturer's declaration of conformity with applicable EU legislation, not a third-party award; the European Commission explains the route.

CE 标志是制造商对适用欧盟法规的符合性声明，并非第三方颁发；欧盟委员会说明了具体路径。

02. Hold a signed Declaration of Conformity持有签署的符合性声明

A Declaration of Conformity lists the directives, harmonised standards and signatory, and must be available to market surveillance authorities.

符合性声明列明指令、协调标准与签署人，须可随时提交市场监管机构。

03. Know when a notified body is required判断何时需要公告机构

Most low-voltage electronics are self-declared, but certain categories require a notified body assessment — check the directive before assuming.

多数低压电子产品可自我声明，但部分品类须经 公告机构评定——先查指令再下结论。

04. Treat UKCA as a separate route把 UKCA 作为独立路径

The UKCA marking guidance sets out its own rules for Great Britain; it is not automatically interchangeable with CE.

UKCA 标志指南规定了大不列颠市场的独立规则，不能与 CE 自动互换。

05. Cover substance restrictions explicitly明确覆盖限用物质

RoHS restricts specific substances in EEE and its binding text is Directive 2011/65/EU; EPA and REACH-style material declarations add further obligations.

RoHS 限制电子电气设备中的特定物质，法律文本为 2011/65/EU 指令；EPA 与 REACH 类材料声明带来进一步义务。

06. Document radio and EMC compliance记录射频与电磁兼容合规

Wireless products using Wi-Fi or Bluetooth carry radio and EMC obligations; the ETSI standards set the European test basis.

采用 Wi-Fi 或 蓝牙的无线产品负有射频与 EMC 义务；ETSI 标准给出欧洲测试依据。

07. Use the CB Scheme to avoid repeat testing用 CB 体系避免重复测试

The IECEE CB Scheme lets one test report and certificate support national approvals across participating countries, shortening multi-market launches.

IECEE CB 体系可用一份测试报告与证书支持多成员国转证，缩短多市场上市周期。

08. Work from the IEC standards catalogue以 IEC 标准目录为基准

IEC and the IEC Webstore are the primary sources for the test standards a report should cite; check the edition year in the certificate.

IEC 与 IEC 标准目录是测试报告应引用标准的主要来源；核对证书中的标准版本年份。

09. Match the certificate to the exact model让证书对应具体型号

A UL certification or CB report covers only the models and constructions listed; a renamed SKU still needs to appear in the scope.

UL 认证或 CB 报告只覆盖所列型号与结构；换个 SKU 名称仍需列入覆盖范围。

10. Keep the evidence pack current保持证据包持续有效

Component changes, firmware changes and standard revisions can invalidate old reports; conformity assessment practice favours version-controlled evidence, not a one-time PDF.

元器件、固件变更或标准改版可能使旧报告失效；合格评定通行做法强调版本受控的证据，而非一次性 PDF。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

CE marking

The manufacturer's declaration that a product meets applicable EU harmonised legislation; it is not a third-party certificate.

制造商声明产品符合欧盟适用法规，并非第三方证书。

UKCA marking

The UK conformity marking used for goods placed on the Great Britain market, with its own guidance and route.

投放英国（大不列颠）市场使用的合格评定标志，规则与 CE 不同。

FCC equipment authorization

The U.S. regime requiring radio-frequency devices and digital equipment to meet emission and labelling rules before marketing.

美国对射频与数字设备的电磁发射及标签合规要求。

RoHS

The EU Directive 2011/65/EU restricting specific hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment.

欧盟 2011/65/EU 指令，限制电子电气设备中的特定有害物质。

REACH

The EU regulation on registration, evaluation and authorisation of chemicals, relevant to materials declarations on hardware.

欧盟化学品注册评估与授权法规，影响硬件材料声明。

CB Scheme

The IECEE system allowing a single test report and certificate to support national approvals across participating countries.

IECEE 体系，用一份测试报告与证书支持多国转证。

Declaration of Conformity

A signed document in which the manufacturer lists the directives and standards a product complies with and takes responsibility.

制造商签署、列明产品所符合指令与标准并承担责任的文件。

Notified body

An EU-designated conformity assessment organisation required for certain high-risk product categories.

欧盟指定的合格评定机构，部分高风险品类必须介入。

Harmonised standard

An EN standard cited in the EU Official Journal that gives a presumption of conformity with a directive.

被欧盟官方公报引用、可推定符合指令要求的 EN 标准。

GTIN

Global Trade Item Number: the GS1 identifier behind retail barcodes and marketplace listing keys.

GS1 全球贸易项目代码，是零售条码与平台商品标识的基础。
