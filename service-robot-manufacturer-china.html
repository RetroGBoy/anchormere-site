Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Chinese robot exports grew rapidly through 2026, with commercial cleaning, delivery and inspection robots moving from trade-show demonstrations into batch orders, and humanoid and quadruped platforms growing even faster off a small base. Most buyers at that stage are not looking for a single finished robot; they need a supply partner who can hold tolerances on a chassis, qualify a motor and sensor module set, and keep sample-to-mass-production consistency across a mechanical bill of materials that can run to hundreds of parts. Anchormere coordinates that physical build in the Greater Bay Area: sheet-metal and CNC frames, injection-moulded housings, wheel and drive modules, wiring harnesses, sensor brackets, battery integration and final assembly and functional test. We do not claim to supply robot navigation, SLAM or control algorithms — those stay with the buyer's software and motion-control partners — and we do not present prototypes as mass-production-ready. What we offer is a tooling-and-assembly route with a pilot batch, an inspection checklist and one accountable coordinator, so a buyer can move from a demo unit to a repeatable production lot without rebuilding the supplier base.

Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A robot is a tolerance chain: a chassis that is 0.5 mm out at the caster mount shows up later as docking failure and wheel wear, not as an obvious visual defect.

Process & technical notes

  • Chassis and caster mounts are checked on a fixture before any harness or module is fitted, so datum drift is caught early.
  • Harnesses are built with keyed connectors and labelled routing, then continuity-tested as a subassembly.
  • Sensor brackets are assembled with a calibration fixture so LiDAR and camera alignment is repeatable across units.

Buyer checklist

  • Define who owns SLAM, fleet software and safety certification before the mechanical drawing is frozen.
  • Require a design-for-manufacture review on prototype-derived parts; demo units are usually not tooled for volume.
  • Ask for end-of-line test criteria with the drawing, including docking success rate and runtime threshold.

QC & inspection focus

  • Each pilot unit runs the full end-of-line sequence and the results are filed against the golden sample.
  • Wheel, caster and gearbox modules are batch-traceable so a wear issue can be isolated to one supplier lot.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you supply the robot's navigation or control software?

No. We build and test the physical platform; SLAM, navigation, fleet software and motion control stay with your software and motion-control partners. The boundary is stated at RFQ.

What is the MOQ for a robot platform?

500 pcs for a pilot batch. A complex platform with new tooling needs a longer sample phase, which we quote separately.

Can you take a demo prototype into mass production?

We can run a design-for-manufacture review, re-tool where needed and build a pilot batch, but we do not present a prototype as production-ready. Sample-to-mass-production consistency is verified against an approved golden sample.

Which parts of the robot do you actually make?

Sheet-metal and CNC frames, injection housings, wheel and drive modules, harnesses, sensor brackets, battery integration, final assembly and end-of-line functional test.

How do you check a robot before shipment?

End-of-line tests cover drive, obstacle detection, auto-docking, runtime and noise, with results filed per batch against the golden sample.

Can you build a humanoid robot for us?

We supply and integrate the mechanical and electromechanical build — frames, housings, harnesses, drive and sensor modules, assembly and test. Actuator control, balance algorithms and system software remain with your specialist partners.

Why do you insist on a pilot batch?

Because a demo unit is usually hand-built. A 500-pc pilot is where tooling variation, harness fit and docking repeatability actually appear, and it is far cheaper to fix them there than after volume tooling.
