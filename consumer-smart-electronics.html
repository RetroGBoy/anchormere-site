What we supply

供应清单

Mobile & tablet assemblies

Housings, frames, batteries (cell sourcing), displays, cameras and PCBA routed across Shenzhen and Dongguan. Finished-device assembly and spare-part kits both handled.

PCBA & box-build

Prototype-to-volume printed circuit boards, SMT assembly and full box-build. We manage the build; firmware and circuit design stay with your engineering team or a specialist partner.

Enclosures & housings

Injection-molded and CNC housings, frames and brackets for consumer devices — aluminum, ABS/PC and magnesium alloys.

Display & touch modules

LCD/OLED panels, touch glass, cover lenses and bonding-ready modules sourced from the region's display supply chain.

Connectors & modules

Board-to-board, wire-to-board, RF and USB-C connectors, plus sensor and wireless modules.

Audio & smart-speaker parts

Speaker drivers, mic arrays, acoustic enclosures and grilles for earbuds, headphones and smart speakers.

Frequently asked about consumer electronics

消费电子常见问答

Do you build finished devices or just parts?
Both. We assemble finished consumer devices and also supply individual components. We don't own the circuit or firmware design — we integrate it. Send a sample or BOM; we quote the build and the sourcing.
What is your minimum order quantity?
500 pieces per item for standard parts; finished-device runs are quoted per project. Mixed-SKU consolidation helps reach the MOQ in a single shipment.
Can you handle powered products and certifications?
Yes. We assemble and integrate powered products — the build, test and packing — while design responsibility and market certifications (CE, FCC, etc.) are scoped per project and supported by our documentation package.
