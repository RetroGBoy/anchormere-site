Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

New-energy exports from China expanded sharply through 2026, with portable power stations and small residential storage moving from optional accessories to everyday essentials in markets with unstable grids. For a buyer, the hard part is not finding a cell supplier — it is integrating cells, BMS, inverter PCBA, thermal management and a certified enclosure into a unit that ships legally as dangerous goods and performs the same in the thousandth cycle as in the first. Anchormere coordinates that integration in the Greater Bay Area: we source cells and modules to the buyer's approved list, assemble packs with the specified BMS, integrate inverter and charging PCBA, build sheet-metal or injection enclosures, and run functional and safety checks before packing. We do not manufacture cells, we do not issue UN38.3 or IEC test reports, and we do not claim certifications we have not verified; test reports and dangerous-goods documentation are coordinated with the buyer's nominated lab and freight forwarder. What we own is a traceable pack build, a consistent export document set and a single accountable coordinator for a category where paperwork is as important as the hardware.

Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A battery pack is a traceability problem: capacity and safety behaviour are decided by cell lot, busbar torque and BMS calibration, none of which is visible once the enclosure is closed.

Process & technical notes

  • Cell lots are recorded against each pack serial before assembly, so a capacity outlier can be traced to one incoming lot.
  • Busbar and connector torque values are logged per unit and the BMS protection thresholds are verified on the assembled pack.
  • A thermal path check is run at rated load before the enclosure is sealed, since a gap shows up only under sustained output.

Buyer checklist

  • Nominate the cell vendor and BMS in the drawing; substituting either changes the test and certification basis.
  • Confirm who obtains UN38.3, IEC and the DG declaration before the production slot, not at booking.
  • Require pack-level traceability and per-unit torque and capacity records with the shipment.

QC & inspection focus

  • Every pack runs capacity, over-current / over-temperature trip and insulation checks before packing.
  • Cycle sampling is taken from the pilot batch and filed with the cell-lot record for later comparison.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you manufacture the battery cells?

No. We source cells and modules to your approved vendor list and assemble the pack with the specified BMS. Cell manufacturing and cell-level certification stay with the cell maker.

Can you provide UN38.3 or IEC test reports?

We do not issue test reports or claim unverified certifications. We coordinate sample submission, pack traceability and dangerous-goods documentation with your nominated lab and forwarder.

What is the MOQ for a portable power station?

500 pcs for a pilot batch on an existing platform. A new enclosure or inverter platform needs a longer sample phase, quoted separately.

How do you keep packs consistent across a batch?

Cells are lot-traceable, busbar and connector torque is recorded, and every pack runs capacity, protection-trip and insulation checks before packing.

Can you handle the dangerous-goods shipping paperwork?

We supply pack data, labels and a consistent commercial invoice and packing list, and coordinate with your forwarder. The carrier and your DG declarant complete the formal declaration.

Do you issue the battery certifications?

No. We assemble and test the pack and provide traceability and documentation support; test reports and certifications are issued by the buyer's nominated lab and the cell maker. We do not claim certifications we have not verified.

Why do you need the cell vendor named in the drawing?

Because the cell lot and BMS define capacity, protection behaviour and the basis of the test report. A substitution invalidates that basis and can change the dangerous-goods classification data.
