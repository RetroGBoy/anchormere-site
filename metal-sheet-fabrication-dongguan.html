Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Sheet metal is a tolerance-chain problem: laser cut edge, bend K-factor and coating build-up each eat into the final assembly fit.

Process & technical notes

  • Bend allowance uses a measured K-factor for the actual material lot, not a handbook table, to hold ±0.2 mm on multi-bend enclosures.
  • Laser / punch nesting is planned around grain direction and flatness; a poorly nested part warps after welding.
  • Powder-coat or anodise build-up is compensated in the pre-coat dimensions so threaded inserts still fit.

Buyer checklist

  • Send a flat pattern or a 3D model with bend radii, material grade and gauge; gauge changes the bend deduction.
  • Specify which faces are cosmetic and require no scratches after coating — it determines handling and packaging cost.
  • Confirm insert, weld and rivet standards, and whether a painted or bare assembly is the acceptance state.

QC & inspection focus

  • In-process bend-angle check on the first piece of every setup, plus a final assembly-fit gauge on the coated part.
  • Coating thickness and adhesion cross-hatch test sampled per lot; salt-spray hours agreed in the RFQ.

Bend allowance, cut edges and pretreatment

折弯补偿、切割面与前处理

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • Bend allowance and K-factor depend on the actual tool radius and material batch, not on a generic chart; a first-article flat pattern should be corrected from measured parts before a laser nest is released.
  • Laser-cut edges on stainless and aluminium carry a heat-affected zone and a dross direction; specify which face is cosmetic and whether a deburr, tumble or passivation step is required before powder coat.
  • Powder-coat adhesion on aluminium needs a proper pretreatment (chromate-free conversion or equivalent); adhesion failure months later is usually a pretreatment issue, not a powder issue.
  • Weld distortion on thin-gauge enclosures is controlled by fixture design and weld sequence; specify flatness and hole-position tolerance on the finished weldment, not on the individual cut parts.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer sends a 3D STEP with no flat pattern and asks for a quote in 24 hours. We can quote a budget, but an accurate price needs bend radii, hardware list and finish spec; we normally return a short RFQ checklist with the budget so the number does not get revised twice.
