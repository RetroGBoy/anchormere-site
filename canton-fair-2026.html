Canton Fair 2026: a buyer's guide to the world's biggest trade fair

广交会 2026：全球最大贸易展买家指南

The China Import and Export Fair — the Canton Fair — is held twice a year in Guangzhou and remains the largest comprehensive trade fair in the country. For overseas buyers, it is the single most efficient place to sample the Greater Bay Area and Pearl River Delta supply base in person.

What each phase covers

The fair is split into three phases so buyers can plan a focused visit:

  • Phase 1 — Advanced manufacturing: electronics & home appliances, lighting, hardware & tools, new energy, vehicles & two-wheelers, industrial automation, power equipment, new materials.
  • Phase 2 — Quality home life: consumer ceramics, housewares, gifts & decorations, building & furniture, gardening, sanitary ware.
  • Phase 3 — Better life: textiles & apparel, shoes & bags, office & school supplies, food, health & recreation, medical devices, pets.

How buyers use it (and where an integrator helps)

Before

Register the buyer badge and visa letter early, pick one phase, send your spec to 8–12 target exhibitors, and book Shenzhen or Dongguan factory visits for the two days after your phase.

During

Walk the halls by process, not by booth size. Ask for the production address and the line that will run your order; collect the legal entity name on the card, not the booth name.

After

Visit two or three factories, keep the samples, and compare landed cost including tooling and packaging. Ship as one consolidated lot — three separate shipments cost more than the unit-price difference.

What you meet at a booth is sales, not the line. For the verification sequence, see six steps to find a manufacturer; if you have not decided between platforms and factories, start with the OEM marketplace guide.

FAQ

常见问题

When is the Canton Fair in 2026?

The 139th session runs 15 April to 5 May 2026 (Phase 1: 15–19 Apr, Phase 2: 23–27 Apr, Phase 3: 1–5 May). The 140th runs 15 October to 4 November 2026 (Phase 1: 15–19 Oct, Phase 2: 23–27 Oct, Phase 3: 31 Oct–4 Nov).

Which phase should electronics buyers attend?

Phase 1. It covers electronics and home appliances, lighting, hardware and tools, new energy, vehicles, industrial automation, power equipment and new materials — the processes behind most consumer hardware.

Do I need an invitation to attend?

Buyers register through the official buyer system at cantonfair.org.cn, which issues the buyer badge and the invitation letter that visa applications usually ask for. We do not issue invitations.

Can I visit factories during the fair?

Yes. Shenzhen and Dongguan are roughly 30–60 minutes from Guangzhou South by high-speed rail, so most buyers book factory visits for the two days after their phase rather than during it.

Is attending the fair enough to choose a supplier?

No. A booth proves the company can afford a booth. Licence scope, a paid sample from the production line and an audit are still what decide whether the order will repeat.
