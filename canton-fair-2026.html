Canton Fair 2026: a buyer's guide to the world's biggest trade fair

The China Import and Export Fair — the Canton Fair — is held twice a year in Guangzhou and remains the largest comprehensive trade fair in the country. For overseas buyers, it is the single most efficient place to sample the Greater Bay Area and Pearl River Delta supply base in person.

At a glance (2026)

EventChina Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair), 139th (Spring) & 140th (Autumn)
139th SpringPhase 1: 15–19 Apr · Phase 2: 23–27 Apr · Phase 3: 1–5 May 2026
140th AutumnPhase 1: 15–19 Oct · Phase 2: 23–27 Oct · Phase 3: 31 Oct – 4 Nov 2026
VenueCanton Fair Complex, 382 Yuejiang Zhong Road, Haizhu, Guangzhou
Scale~32,000 exhibitors · ~1.55 million m² · 55 exhibition zones
OrganizersMinistry of Commerce & Guangdong Provincial Government; operated by China Foreign Trade Centre

What each phase covers

The fair is split into three phases so buyers can plan a focused visit:

  • Phase 1 — Advanced manufacturing: electronics & home appliances, lighting, hardware & tools, new energy, vehicles & two-wheelers, industrial automation, power equipment, new materials.
  • Phase 2 — Quality home life: consumer ceramics, housewares, gifts & decorations, building & furniture, gardening, sanitary ware.
  • Phase 3 — Better life: textiles & apparel, shoes & bags, office & school supplies, food, health & recreation, medical devices, pets.

How buyers use it (and where an integrator helps)

The Canton Fair is excellent for discovery, but most serious production does not happen at the booth — it happens at the factory, in the cluster, with the right QC and consolidation. That is where Anchormere adds value:

  • Pre-fair shortlist — tell us your category and target spec; we map the relevant exhibitors and the underlying GBA factories behind them.
  • Factory vetting & sampling — we visit and qualify workshops the fair cannot show you (small-batch, mixed-SKU makers).
  • Consolidation & export docs — one shipment, one accountable partner, full documentation, instead of juggling dozens of booths.
