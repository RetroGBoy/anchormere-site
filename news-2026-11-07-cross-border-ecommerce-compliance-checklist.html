Cross-border ecommerce sourcing: the 10-point compliance checklist

跨境电商供应链采购：10 项合规清单

Most cross-border sourcing problems are not manufacturing problems — they are classification, marking and paperwork problems that surface at the border or on the marketplace. This briefing walks through ten checks we run before a Greater Bay Area order ships, with the official sources behind each one so your team can verify them independently.

跨境电商采购的多数问题并非出在制造，而是出在归类、标识与单证——往往到清关或平台入仓时才暴露。本简报按我们发货前的十项检查逐条拆解，并附上每项对应的官方来源，便于你的团队自行核验。

The ten checks

01. Classify every SKU before you request a quote报价前先为每个 SKU 归类

Assign a six-digit HS code to each item and record the destination-country extension; the same finished good can fall under different lines depending on material and function.

为每个品项确定 6 位 HS 编码并记录目的国延伸子目；同一成品会因材质与功能落入不同税则行。

02. Look up the real duty line, not an estimate查真实税则行，不用估算值

Check the destination schedule — for the U.S. the HTS gives the statistical suffix and rate — and cross-check general tariff data at the WTO.

查目的国税则：美国用 HTS 查统计后缀与税率，并以 WTO 的关税数据交叉核对。

03. Fix the trade term in the purchase order把贸易术语写进采购订单

State the Incoterms 2020 rule explicitly (for example FOB Shenzhen or DAP Rotterdam); a missing rule is the most common cause of freight and clearance disputes.

在订单中明确 Incoterms 2020 术语（如 FOB 深圳、DAP 鹿特丹）；术语缺失是运费与清关争议最常见的起因。

04. Register the importer identifiers early尽早完成进口方资质登记

EU-bound shipments need a valid EORI number, and low-value consignments may fall under the IOSS VAT route — confirm the scheme before the first parcel ships.

发往欧盟的货物需有效 EORI 号，低值包裹可能适用 IOSS 增值税路径——首票发出前先确认适用方案。

05. Give every listing a stable product identity为每个商品建立稳定标识

Allocate GTINs and keep a clean mapping between your SKU, the factory part number and the marketplace listing ID; mismatched IDs cause suppression at inbound.

分配 GTIN 并维护 SKU、工厂料号与平台商品标识的映射；标识不一致常导致入仓被拒。

06. Confirm destination-market markings确认目的市场标识要求

Great Britain requires its own UKCA marking route, while EU goods follow conformity assessment under the single market rules — the two are not interchangeable.

英国（大不列颠）有独立的 UKCA 标志路径，欧盟货物走 单一市场合格评定，二者不可互用。

07. Clear substance restrictions on materials核查材料限用物质

Electronics and hardware must satisfy the EU RoHS Directive, whose binding legal text is published on EUR-Lex; ask for material declarations per component, not a single blanket certificate.

电子与硬件须符合 欧盟 RoHS 指令，其法律全文见 EUR-Lex；按元器件索取材料声明，而非一张笼统证书。

08. Secure the brand before tooling starts开模前先保护品牌

File trade marks in the destination markets through the USPTO or the WIPO route before you pay for moulds and packaging artwork.

在支付模具与包装设计费之前，先通过 USPTO 或 WIPO 途径在目的市场注册商标。

09. Ship a complete document pack随货配齐单证包

The ITA export guidance lists the core set — commercial invoice, packing list, origin evidence and transport document; we attach it to every consolidated shipment.

ITA 出口指引列出核心单证：商业发票、装箱单、原产地证明与运输单据；我们随每票整合货附齐。

10. Build traceability into the PO把可追溯性写进订单

Require lot codes and test records aligned with recognised conformity assessment practice so a marketplace or customs query can be answered with evidence, not assurances.

要求按 合格评定通行做法提供批次码与测试记录，使平台或海关问询能用证据回应，而非口头保证。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

HS code

A six-digit Harmonized System classification assigned by the World Customs Organization; countries append their own digits for tariff and statistical lines.

世界海关组织制定的 6 位商品编码，各国再延长本国税则子目。

HTS

The Harmonized Tariff Schedule: the destination-country tariff schedule built on the HS, used to look up duty rates and statistical suffixes.

基于 HS 的目的国关税表，用于查询税率与统计后缀。

EORI number

Economic Operators Registration and Identification number: the identifier an importer or exporter needs for customs declarations in the EU.

欧盟进出口商办理海关申报所需的经营者注册识别号。

GTIN

Global Trade Item Number: the GS1 identifier behind retail barcodes and marketplace listing keys.

GS1 全球贸易项目代码，是零售条码与平台商品标识的基础。

Incoterms

The ICC's published trade terms (current edition: Incoterms 2020) that allocate delivery, risk, cost and customs responsibility between seller and buyer.

国际商会发布的贸易术语（现行为 2020 版），划分买卖双方交货、风险、费用与清关责任。

IOSS

Import One-Stop Shop: an EU VAT scheme that lets sellers declare and pay VAT on low-value imported consignments through a single registration.

欧盟进口一站式增值税申报机制，用于低价值包裹的集中申报缴税。

SKU

Stock-keeping unit: the buyer's own identifier for a distinct sellable item, separate from the manufacturer's part number.

买方自定义的可售品项编码，与工厂料号相互独立。

FBA

Fulfilment by Amazon: the marketplace's storage, picking and delivery service, which imposes its own labelling and packaging rules.

亚马逊仓储配送服务，对标签与包装有独立要求。

CE marking

The manufacturer's declaration that a product meets applicable EU harmonised legislation; it is not a third-party certificate.

制造商声明产品符合欧盟适用法规，并非第三方证书。

RoHS

The EU Directive 2011/65/EU restricting specific hazardous substances in electrical and electronic equipment.

欧盟 2011/65/EU 指令，限制电子电气设备中的特定有害物质。
