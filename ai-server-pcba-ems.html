Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

On a large, high-layer board the reflow profile is no longer a single oven recipe: thermal mass varies across the panel, and the BGA corner joints see a different time-above-liquidus than the centre devices.

Process & technical notes

  • A board-specific reflow profile is developed with thermocouples on the largest BGA and on a low-mass corner, then locked; weekly profiler runs catch oven drift.
  • X-ray inspection targets voiding and head-in-pillow on high-pin-count BGA; a single 2D image cannot quantify void area, so the sampling plan and acceptance limit are agreed up front.
  • Cold-plate or heat-sink mounting torque and TIM thickness change the junction temperature; the assembly instruction should state torque sequence and the measured bond-line thickness.

Buyer checklist

  • Provide Gerber/ODB++, a centroid file, an approved BOM with manufacturer part numbers, a test spec and the fixture requirement as one build package.
  • State the IPC-A-610 class plus the functional-test coverage by net or by link, not a blanket 100% test clause.
  • Agree first-pass yield targets, the rework rule and the serial-numbered report format before the pilot build.

QC & inspection focus

  • SPI on 100% of prints, AOI after reflow, X-ray on all high-pin-count BGA and bottom-terminated parts.
  • Run FCT and burn-in with a logged serial number per unit; issue a defect Pareto and a link-training log with each lot.

Large-format reflow, BGA voiding and link-level functional test

大尺寸回流、BGA 空洞与链路级功能测试

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • A locked reflow profile developed with thermocouples on the largest BGA and a low-mass corner is not transferable to a different board thickness; thermal mass, not the oven recipe, sets the joint history.
  • A blanket 100% test clause usually excludes boundary-scan-only nets and high-speed link training; coverage should be defined by link and by net class instead.
  • Cold-plate mounting torque and TIM bond-line thickness change junction temperature as much as the heat-sink choice, so the assembly instruction and the thermal test belong in one build package.
  • Consigned high-pin-count BGA need moisture-sensitive handling with bake records; a lot code missing at goods-in makes a later field failure untraceable.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer's rack fails acceptance intermittently at high ambient temperature. Three vendors each report a clean result: the board house passes impedance, the assembly house passes AOI, and the thermal vendor passes leak test. We normally require one serial-numbered system-level report per unit — link training, sensor and fan checks, and thermal behaviour during burn-in — so the failure can be localised to a unit rather than argued between suppliers.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What makes AI server PCBA different from ordinary SMT?

Board size and mass, high-pin-count BGA, tight thermal-planarity requirements and a functional test that must exercise high-speed links rather than just power-on.

Do you provide IPC-A-610 Class 3 certification?

No. IPC references are public editorial references. The applicable class is stated in the PO and the fabricator's inspection evidence is passed through for verification.

Can you work with consigned components?

Yes. Consigned parts are stored by lot code, moisture-sensitive devices are baked and tracked, and unused stock is reconciled at shipment.

What is included in system-level test?

A test spec agreed at RFQ: firmware load, high-speed link training, sensor and fan checks, thermal behaviour, and a serial-numbered report per unit.

Can a pilot run be smaller than 500 pcs?

Yes. Prototype and pilot builds can start from a handful of units; the 500 pcs per item MOQ applies to production runs, not engineering validation.

Do you do component sourcing or only assembly?

Both. Turnkey sourcing and consigned-component models are supported, with the AVL and alternate-part policy locked before the pilot.
