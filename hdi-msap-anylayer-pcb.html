Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Fine-line yield is set by the patterning method and the dielectric surface, not by the design rule written on the drawing. Below a few mil, mSAP changes both the conductor profile and the inspection method.

Process & technical notes

  • mSAP builds conductors by pattern plating on a thin seed layer; the seed-layer etch uniformity then controls line-width spread more than the exposure tool does.
  • Stacked microvias concentrate thermal-cycle strain at the interface between build-up layers, so the fill copper quality and the interface cleanliness determine field reliability.
  • Via-in-pad planarity affects BGA and fine-pitch assembly; a specified dimple limit must be paired with the measurement method on the same drawing.

Buyer checklist

  • Specify the microvia structure per layer interface (stacked, staggered or skipped) rather than a single HDI stage number.
  • Ask for line/space yield data at the exact minimum width on the drawing, not the fabricator's best-case capability.
  • Define the applicable IPC class and the microvia reliability test method in the PO so both sides inspect to the same criteria.

QC & inspection focus

  • Cross-section microvia chains from each build-up interface and review the fill void percentage against the agreed class.
  • Use AOI tuned for fine-line defects and confirm the seed-layer residue is cleared, since residual seed causes leakage between adjacent traces.

Microvia structure, seed-layer etch and filled-via planarity

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • HDI names an interconnect structure while mSAP names a patterning process; a board can use stacked microvias without mSAP, and fine lines without any microvia at all.
  • Stacked microvias concentrate thermal-cycle strain at the build-up interface; the fill copper void percentage matters more to field reliability than the nominal via diameter.
  • In mSAP, seed-layer etch uniformity sets the line-width spread; an exposure tool capable of 3/3 mil does not by itself guarantee that yield at that rule.
  • A dimple limit on via-in-pad is meaningless without the measurement method and the clamping condition used during final assembly.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer specifies any-layer HDI with 3/3 mil lines to shrink a module, then asks why the quoted lead time is longer than a staged HDI build. Every layer interface now needs its own lamination and laser-drill cycle, and the fine-line yield drops at the minimum width. We normally return two options — any-layer with the full density, and a staged build that keeps the same routing on the critical interfaces — with yield and lead-time figures for each, so the decision is made before tooling.

常见问题

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

What is the difference between HDI and mSAP?

HDI describes the interconnect structure (microvias and build-up layers); mSAP is a patterning process used to reach very fine line/space. A board can be HDI without mSAP.

Are stacked microvias a reliability risk?

They need tighter process control than staggered vias. Public standards discuss microvia reliability; the fabricator should evidence its own IST or thermal-cycling results for that structure.

Do you hold IPC certification for HDI builds?

No. IPC references are public editorial references. Conformance evidence and the applicable class are requested from the fabricator and confirmed in the PO.

What files are needed to quote an any-layer build?

A build-up drawing with layer count, microvia structure per interface, line/space minimum, filled-via planarity requirement and the target IPC class.

Does any-layer always cost more than a staged HDI build?

Usually, because every layer interface needs a lamination and laser-drill cycle. A staged build can be cheaper if routing allows.

Can you source HDI without assembly?

Yes. Bare-board-only enquiries are consolidated with the fabricator's qualification report and shipped to the buyer's nominated EMS.
