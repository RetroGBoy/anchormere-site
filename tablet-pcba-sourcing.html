Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Tablet and phone PCBA is a high-density interconnect job: BGA, eMMC/UFS and display bonding tolerances decide yield long before assembly starts.

Process & technical notes

  • HDI stack-up and via-in-pad design are reviewed in DFM before Gerber release to avoid delamination during reflow.
  • eMMC/UFS firmware pre-programming and SoC matching are verified on a pilot lot before the full BOM is committed.
  • Display FPC bonding and backlight driver current are checked as a paired station to prevent mura and early dimming.

Buyer checklist

  • Send a complete BOM with approved alternates and lifecycle status — EOL memory parts are the most common delay.
  • Specify ICT / FCT coverage and the acceptable AOI false-call rate in the RFQ, not after first article.
  • Clarify whether the display module and battery are consigned or turnkey; liability for dead pixels must be assigned.

QC & inspection focus

  • SPI solder-paste inspection plus X-ray on all BGA sites; AOI alone cannot see head-in-pillow defects.
  • FCT with thermal soak and a documented yield Pareto; any lot below the agreed first-pass yield is held.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you offer turnkey (consignment + assembly) PCBA?

Yes. We do BOM sourcing, SMT/DIP, testing and box-build as a one-stop service.

What is your small-batch MOQ?

From 500 pcs per SKU; prototypes and pilot runs are available.

How do you control component quality?

Authorised-distributor sourcing, incoming inspection and AOI/X-ray/ICT test with full traceability.

Can you handle camera and display module integration?

Yes, including camera, touch and LCD/OLED module bonding.

Do you support consigned components?

Yes. Consigned SoCs, memory and displays are logged by lot code, stored separately and reconciled against yield reports at shipment.

What is a realistic first-pass yield for a tablet PCBA pilot?

A controlled pilot typically lands in the low-to-mid 90s percent; we agree the target in writing before the run and report the Pareto by defect class.
