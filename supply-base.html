By cluster · 按产业带

One integrator across nine specialized cities

九大专业城市，一个集成商

We don't run a single factory — we route your order to the right Greater Bay Area cluster, then take single-point responsibility for what ships. Here is what each cluster is known for.

Shenzhen — electronics & communications

The prototyping and electronics capital of the region. Strong in low-volume, high-mix production and fast iteration.

PCB & PCBA

Prototype to volume printed circuit boards, SMT assembly, box-build.

Connectors & terminals

Board-to-board, wire-to-board, RF and industrial connectors; custom contact pins.

Enclosures & housings

Aluminum and steel electronics enclosures, panel-mount cases, DIN-rail boxes.

Electronic modules

Power modules, control boards, sensor assemblies, IoT nodes.

Heatsinks & thermal

Extruded, bonded and stamped heatsinks, thermal interface kits.

Cable & wire (non-live)

Custom looms, overmolded leads, labeling and strain relief — assembly only, no live harness under load.

Dongguan — sheet metal, plastics & cabling

The workshop of the Pearl River Delta: dense metal and plastic supply, fast tooling, strong cabling ecosystem.

Sheet-metal structures

Brackets, frames, cabinets, panels — laser cut, bent, welded, powder-coated.

Injection-molded parts

Housings, jigs, clips, covers; tooling carried by buyer where applicable.

Cable management

J-hooks, D-rings, ladder tray, ties, spiral wrap, ground straps, labels.

Rack accessories

Blanking panels, cage nuts & screws, shelves, brackets, vented panels.

Fasteners & consumables

Sealing strips, foam tape, grounding kits, torque-marked hardware.

Stamped & turned parts

Small metal stampings, CNC-turned fittings, spacers, standoffs.

Foshan & Zhongshan — metal fabrication

Heavy metalworking and hardware heartland: aluminum profiles, welded structures, finished hardware.

Aluminum profiles

Extruded sections, frames, rails, T-slot and custom profiles.

Welded structures

Frames, enclosures, machine bases, brackets — MIG/TIG and robotic weld.

Stamped hardware

Hinges, latches, handles, mounts, standard and custom stampings.

Castings

Die-cast and sand-cast components, post-machined and finished.

Surface finishing

Powder coating, anodizing, plating, passivation, silk-screen.

Lighting hardware

Fixtures, mounts, poles, enclosures for commercial and industrial lighting.

Huizhou — energy storage & PV

A growing hub for battery and solar hardware, with matured enclosure and structure supply.

Battery enclosures

Cabinets and racks for residential and commercial storage (structure only).

PV mounting

Rail, clamps, roof and ground mounts, tracker hardware.

Busbar & supports

Insulated busbar supports, brackets, isolators (non-live structure).

Inverter enclosures

Sheet-metal and cast enclosures for string and hybrid inverters.

Cable & containment

Tray, conduit, ties and labels for solar and storage sites.

Consumables kits

Sealing, grounding and fastening sets packaged per site.

Guangzhou & surrounding — finishing, packaging & logistics

The trade and fulfillment layer: finishing, export packaging, inspection and consolidated shipping.

Export packaging

Cartons, foam, pallets, vacuum and anti-rust packing per destination.

Inspection & QC

In-line and final inspection, third-party audit coordination.

Consolidation

Multi-SKU bundling, one document package, one shipment.
