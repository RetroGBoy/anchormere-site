Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A module board is a microwave component that happens to carry digital data. At these rates the transition geometry, the gold-finger launch and the reference-plane discontinuity can dominate over the trace loss itself.

Process & technical notes

  • mSAP is used because the fine line/space and smooth conductor profile reduce both loss and impedance spread; the seed-layer etch must be tightly controlled to keep the profile consistent.
  • Gold-finger plating thickness and the edge bevel affect insertion loss and mating durability; a hard-gold spec without a bevel drawing leaves the launch geometry undefined.
  • Embedded silicon capacitors shorten the power delivery path for the optical engine, but they add a lamination step and a new void-inspection requirement.

Buyer checklist

  • Specify insertion loss and return loss per channel with the measurement method, coupon design and the frequency range of interest.
  • State the host interface standard and the optical engine package so the breakout region can be reviewed before tooling.
  • Ask for S-parameter data on the exact laminate lot, since a grade-level datasheet does not capture lot-to-lot Dk spread.

QC & inspection focus

  • Measure VNA S-parameters on the agreed coupon per lot and compare against the channel budget before assembly release.
  • Inspect gold-finger plating thickness, bevel geometry and edge burrs, since a launch defect is rarely caught by electrical test alone.

Insertion-loss budget, launch geometry and S-parameter evidence

插损预算、馈入几何与 S 参数证据

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • At module data rates the gold-finger launch and the reference-plane transition can consume as much loss as several centimetres of trace, so a material upgrade alone rarely closes the budget.
  • mSAP is chosen for smooth conductor profile and fine line/space, but the seed-layer etch must be controlled or line-width spread shows up as impedance ripple.
  • Embedded silicon capacitors shorten the optical-engine power path, yet they add a lamination cycle and require void inspection that a standard bare-board test does not cover.
  • A grade-level laminate datasheet cannot capture lot-to-lot Dk spread; two lots of the same grade can produce measurably different S-parameters at the band edge.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer targets a tight insertion loss for a 1.6T module and asks for a better laminate, while the largest measured discontinuity sits at the gold-finger launch. We normally ask for a de-embedded S-parameter report separating the launch from the trace, then present two costed options — a material upgrade and a launch-geometry revision — so the budget is spent where the measurement says it matters.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What changes between 800G and 1.6T module boards?

Public 2026 reporting describes a layer-count increase and tighter insertion-loss budgets, often with embedded capacitors. The exact stack-up depends on the optical engine and the host channel.

Do you guarantee a specific insertion loss?

The loss budget is a measured requirement with a defined coupon and method. The target is agreed at RFQ and reported per lot, not assumed from a material datasheet.

Are IEEE 802.3 and OIF documents your certifications?

No. They are public editorial references for the interface standard. Conformance evidence comes from the board fabricator and the module maker's own compliance testing.

What is needed for a quote?

An RF stack-up, insertion-loss and return-loss targets by channel, gold-finger finish and thickness, embedded-component requirement and the applicable test method.

Is mSAP mandatory for every 800G board?

No. It depends on line/space and loss requirements. Some designs reach the target with conventional patterning on a better laminate.

Can you quote the board without optical components?

Yes. Bare-board-only enquiries are consolidated with the S-parameter report and shipped to the buyer's nominated module assembler.
