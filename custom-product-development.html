How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Custom development is a stage-gate process: EVT proves the circuit, DVT proves the design, PVT proves the line — skipping a gate moves the cost into warranty.

What we do, step by step

  • EVT builds a small engineering batch to validate the schematic, thermal behaviour and firmware before any industrial design is frozen.
  • DVT units use production-intent materials and tooling and go through drop, thermal, EMC and reliability testing.
  • PVT is a full-speed pilot on the actual line to prove yield, fixture coverage and the assembly work instructions.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Agree the stage-gate deliverables and sign-off owner in writing at kickoff.
  • Name the target certification list and test lab early; DVT samples should be the ones submitted.
  • Budget for at least one tooling correction loop between DVT and PVT.

How we verify & report

  • Each gate closes with a documented test report, an open-issue list and a signed sample.
  • Design-change traceability from the issue list through to the released drawing revision.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can you start from just an idea or sketch?

Yes. We drive industrial design, electronics, firmware and tooling from a sketch, sample or concept.

How long does a prototype take?

Typically 10–15 days for a functional prototype.

Do you handle tooling and private molds?

Yes. Tooling, multi-cavity molds and private mold development are part of the program.

How is my IP protected during development?

NNN / confidentiality, dual-sourcing on key items, and limited drawing release per PO.

Can you start from just an idea or a sketch?

Yes. We begin with a feasibility note covering architecture, rough BOM, certification path and cost band, then move to EVT only after you approve that scope.

Who owns the design IP at the end of development?

Ownership is defined at kickoff; by default the drawings and firmware produced for your project are handed over with a documented file list at PVT close.
