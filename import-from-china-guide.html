  • CBP ruling N357652: e-bike origin is Thailand, not the China assembly siteCBP 裁定 N357652：电动自行车原产地为泰国，而非中国组装地

    In ruling N357652 (2 February 2026), U.S. Customs and Border Protection determined the country of origin of three Aventon e-bike models — Aventure, Level and Pace — to be Thailand for trade-remedy and marking purposes. The frames were wholly produced in Thailand from Chinese and Thai raw materials, then shipped to China for final assembly with components made in China, Indonesia and Vietnam. CBP applied the substantial-transformation test.

    在 2026 年 2 月 2 日的 N357652 裁定中，美国海关与边境保护局（CBP）认定 Aventon 三款电动自行车（Aventure、Level、Pace）在贸易救济与标记意义上的原产地为泰国：车架由中泰原材料在泰国完整制造，再运至中国，与中国、印尼、越南产部件总装。CBP 适用的是「实质性改变」判定标准。

    Buyer takeaway · 采购启示：Assembly location is not the same as country of origin. For tariff-exposed categories, obtain a written origin analysis — or a CBP binding ruling — before committing tooling.

    Source URL: https://rulings.cbp.gov/ruling/N357652 · U.S. CBP CROSS Ruling N357652 · 2026-02-02

  • Two defensible HTS readings, about US$370 per unit apart两个都说得通的 HTS 归类，单台税差约 370 美元

    A 2026 worked example compares two defensible HTS subheadings for a stand-on electric scooter assembled in China and entered on 1 May 2026: 8703.80.00.20 (electric passenger vehicle, effective duty roughly 128%) versus 8711.60.00.90 (electric cycle, roughly 35%). On a US$400 declared value the difference is about US$370 per unit — around US$1.85M a year at 5,000 units — and the analysis recommends requesting a CBP binding ruling rather than self-classifying.

    一份 2026 年的算例比较了中国组装站立式电动滑板车在 2026 年 5 月 1 日申报时两个都可行的 HTS 子目：8703.80.00.20（电动乘用车，综合税负约 128%）与 8711.60.00.90（电动自行车／摩托车，约 35%）。按 400 美元申报价，单台相差约 370 美元，5,000 台／年对应约 185 万美元敞口；分析建议申请 CBP 约束性裁定，而非自行归类。

    Buyer takeaway · 采购启示：When two HTS readings are both defensible, quantify the exposure and get a binding ruling before the first shipment — not after a CBP inquiry arrives.

    Source URL: https://tandom.ai/resources/how-to-request-cbp-binding-ruling · Tandom (worked HTS example) · 2026-05-01

How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Importing is a document and cost chain: HS code, Incoterms 2020, duty, freight and the payment schedule must be agreed before the PO, not at the port.

What we do, step by step

  • Incoterms 2020 defines exactly where cost and risk transfer — EXW, FOB, CIF and DAP put very different duties on the buyer.
  • The HS code drives duty rate, origin marking and any licence or conformity requirement at destination.
  • A full export document package (commercial invoice, packing list, B/L or AWB, certificate of origin, test reports) is assembled before the container is sealed.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Confirm the Incoterm and the named place in the contract, and who books freight and insurance.
  • Ask your broker to pre-classify the HS code and check for anti-dumping or quota measures.
  • Align the 30% deposit / 70% before shipment schedule with your cash flow and inspection rights.

How we verify & report

  • Document pack cross-checked against the packing list and the commercial invoice before release.
  • Carton marks, gross/net weight and piece count verified against the booking.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What are the basic steps to import from China?

Define your spec, find and verify a supplier, quote and sample, contract on the right Incoterms, run QC, arrange freight and customs clearance, then pay the balance after QC passes.

Which Incoterm should a beginner use?

FOB Shenzhen is common: the seller delivers to the port, you control freight and insurance. EXW shifts all logistics to you; DDP shifts everything to the seller but costs more.

How do I estimate landed cost?

Unit price plus freight, insurance, import duty, VAT and last-mile delivery. Always get the HS code right — it drives duty.

How do I find and verify a supplier?

Start on B2B platforms, then verify the operating entity, business license and a recorded shop-floor video. A buying office can do this on your behalf.

What documents do I need to clear customs?

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, HS code and a clean packing list. Your supplier or freight forwarder prepares these.

Which Incoterm should a first-time importer choose?

FOB with a named Chinese port is the common starting point: the supplier handles export clearance and you control the main carriage, but whatever you choose must be written into the contract with a named place.

Do you provide the export documents?

Yes. Every shipment leaves with a full export document package, and multi-SKU consolidated orders share one coordinated set rather than separate paperwork per workshop.
