Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Two importers buying the same factory output can pay very different duty depending on how the article is described and classified. Since 2026 every commercial shipment needs a correct 10-digit classification and a formal or informal entry, so the Harmonized System code is no longer a paperwork detail: it decides the rate stack, the exemption tests and sometimes whether a category is profitable at all. Anchormere does not act as a customs broker and never issues a binding classification or duty ruling. What we do is prepare the export-side evidence your licensed broker needs — an unambiguous product description, material composition, BOM, intended use, photographs, net and gross weights, and a consistent commercial invoice and packing list — and keep those descriptions identical across SKUs, cartons and origins. That removes the most common cause of reclassification delays: a factory invoice that says one thing and a carton label that says another. Rates, exemption tests and rules of origin are confirmed by your broker against the official tariff schedule in force at entry.

How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Classification is decided on the article as described, not on the supplier's catalogue name. The export-side job is to make that description precise, evidenced and identical everywhere it appears.

What we do, step by step

  • The product description is written from material composition and intended use, not from a marketing name or a category label.
  • Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and carton marks are generated from one source description so wording cannot drift.
  • Photographs, BOM and weight data are filed with the document pack and re-issued whenever a specification changes.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Ask your broker to confirm the classification before the production slot, because a material change can move the article to another line.
  • Check that the description on the commercial invoice matches the carton label and the packing list word for word.
  • Treat rates and exemption tests as time-sensitive: review them at every order, not once a year.

How we verify & report

  • A three-way check compares invoice value, packing-list quantity and carton manifest before the container is sealed.
  • Any specification revision triggers a re-issue of the classification data sheet so the broker never works from a stale BOM.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can you tell me the HTS code for my product?

We do not issue binding classifications. We prepare the description, material, BOM, use and weight data so your licensed broker can classify it against the official schedule. A code guessed from a factory invoice is the most common source of delays.

Why does the same product sometimes get different duty?

Rate stacks depend on classification, origin and the exemption tests in force on the day of entry. Small differences in material composition or intended use can move an article to a different line.

What documents does my broker need from the factory?

A commercial invoice and packing list with identical descriptions, material composition, BOM, intended use, product photos, net/gross weights and carton manifest. We keep all of these consistent in one pack.

Do you handle rules of origin or claim preferences?

We supply the manufacturing and sourcing data your broker needs to assess origin, but we do not make the claim. Preference eligibility is a legal determination for the importer and its broker.

What happens if the invoice and carton label disagree?

The broker may have to re-describe the goods, which delays entry and can change the rate. We cross-check invoice, packing list, CO and carton marks against each other before sealing the container.

Do you provide a binding tariff ruling?

No. Binding rulings and classifications come from the customs authority or your licensed broker. We provide the product evidence and keep description wording consistent across all export documents.

Why do you ask for material composition and intended use?

Because those two facts usually decide the tariff line. A marketing name like 'smart band' is not enough for a broker to classify a device that may contain a radio, a battery and a display.
