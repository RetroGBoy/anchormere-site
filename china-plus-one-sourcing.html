China Plus One Sourcing | Dual-Sourcing & Second Supplier Qualification

Diversify by SKU, not by company: keep complex builds in the Greater Bay Area, qualify a second line for simple high-volume items, and map component dependencies before you move.

按 SKU 分散而非按公司搬走：复杂品留在大湾区，简单大批量的品项验证第二产线，转移前先摸清零部件依赖。

Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

China Plus One is real but it is not an exit. Industry surveys through 2025-2026 show most importers moved only part of their volume, and a majority of supply-chain executives named diversification — not relocation — as their leading strategy. The reason is component depth: factories in Vietnam, India or Mexico often still assemble with Chinese subassemblies, so a finished-goods move rarely delivers the independence it appears to promise. Anchormere helps buyers make that decision at SKU level. We map which parts of a bill of materials are truly China-dependent, keep complex multi-material, tight-tolerance and tooling-heavy products in the Greater Bay Area cluster, and qualify a second line for simple, labour-heavy, tariff-sensitive items. We do not sell 'leave China' as a service; we give you a written reason for where each SKU sits, a sample and mass-production consistency check, and one accountable coordinator for both lines. The result is redundancy that survives a duty change or a freight spike without rebuilding your supplier base from scratch.

How the engagement works

合作如何推进

A diversification plan is only as good as its bill of materials: moving final assembly while the subassemblies still ship from China adds a border crossing without removing the dependency.

What we do, step by step

  • Each BOM line is tagged China-dependent, substitutable locally, or dual-sourceable before any origin decision is made.
  • A pilot run at the second line is built to the same drawing and measured against the China sample for tolerance, finish and yield.
  • Product descriptions, HS data and carton manifests are normalised across origins so classification does not drift when a SKU moves.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Classify your catalogue by complexity, not by supplier relationship: tooling depth and tolerance decide where a SKU belongs.
  • Require a written reason for every SKU's origin and re-review it after each duty or freight change.
  • Do not assume a second origin removes China exposure; ask what share of its components are still Chinese.

How we verify & report

  • Pilot-run units from both origins are measured on the same fixture and compared with the approved golden sample.
  • A single carton manifest format is used across origins so inbound receiving and brokerage data stay comparable.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Is everyone moving manufacturing out of China?

No. Most importers diversify a share of volume while China remains central for complex electronics, battery packs, injection-moulded assemblies and tooling-heavy goods. Final assembly often moves while components stay Chinese.

Should I dual-source by company or by SKU?

By SKU. Qualify a second line for the products that are simple, labour-heavy and tariff-sensitive, and keep the complex third of the catalogue in the deepest cluster. Dual-sourcing the whole catalogue usually doubles qualification work for little gain.

What should I check before moving a SKU?

Map the BOM first. If the new origin still imports Chinese subassemblies, you may add lead time and cost without gaining independence. Then compare a pilot run against the China sample for tolerance, finish and yield.

Can Anchormere manage both origins?

We coordinate the China base and qualify a second line, then keep descriptions, carton manifests and export documents consistent across both so your broker and receiver see one set of figures.

How often should the sourcing map be revisited?

Every six months, and immediately after a duty or freight change. Classification and rate schedules move on administrative timelines, so a map that is a year old is usually a stale map.

Will you move all my production out of China?

We do not recommend that. We map component dependencies, keep complex builds in the Greater Bay Area and qualify a second line only where the numbers justify it. The goal is redundancy, not relocation for its own sake.

How do you keep quality consistent between two origins?

Both lines build to the same drawing, both pilot runs are measured against one approved golden sample, and the same inspection checklist and carton manifest format apply. Any deviation is flagged before the production slot is confirmed.
