Logistics

From workshop to your dock

从车间到您的仓库

Multi-SKU orders from different Greater Bay Area workshops are consolidated into one shipment, with one document package. We handle packing, freight coordination and export paperwork so you manage one delivery, not a dozen.

Packaging

Export-grade packaging

出口级包装

Cartons

Standard five-layer export cartons; custom sizes and double-wall cartons for heavy or fragile items.

Protection

Bubble wrap, pearl-cotton (EPE) and dividers per part geometry; moisture-proof bagging where required.

Palletizing

Wooden or plastic pallets, stretch-wrapped; ISPM-15 fumigation certificate provided where wood packaging applies.

Labeling

Carton marks, barcodes and shipping labels produced to your template.

Consolidation

Several SKUs from multiple workshops combined into one shipment to save freight.

Load-out record

Photographed packing, carton count and weight/volume noted per shipment.

Incoterms

Trade terms we work with

我们适用的贸易术语

EXW

Ex-works at the workshop — you handle everything from pickup. Available on request.

FOB Common

Free on board Shenzhen / Guangzhou — our default; we deliver cleared goods onto the vessel.

CIF / CFR

We arrange freight and insurance to your port. Confirm scope before quoting.

DDP

Delivered duty paid to your door — available for selected markets; confirm feasibility first.

Transit risk follows the agreed Incoterms. We are the coordinator, not the carrier — we consolidate and document, but the carrier carries the goods.
