Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Lightweight, compact outdoor gear for short 'micro-getaway' trips was one of the faster-growing ecommerce categories through 2026, and it suits smaller sellers precisely because the tooling is modest and the MOQs are low. The catch is SKU sprawl: a single assortment may mix aluminium folding frames, injection-moulded lamp housings, rotomoulded or EPS coolers, sewn soft goods and small electronics, each from a different workshop. Anchormere consolidates that spread into one accountable build. We source and coordinate aluminium and steel tubing, injection and rotomoulded parts, textile and sewn components, LED lighting modules and retail packaging, then consolidate the assortment into one container with a single commercial invoice, packing list and carton manifest. We quote from 500 pcs per item with mixed-SKU consolidation allowed, which lets a new brand test several products in one shipment instead of committing to a full container of one SKU. We do not claim unverified safety or food-contact certifications; where a cooler liner, a lithium lamp or a load-bearing chair needs a test report, we coordinate with the buyer's nominated lab and keep the report with the document pack.

Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A lightweight outdoor assortment is a consolidation exercise: five workshops produce five very different parts, and the buyer's real risk is a container that arrives with five incompatible document sets.

Process & technical notes

  • Tube frames, moulded parts, sewn goods and lighting modules are quoted as separate line items but consolidated under one carton manifest.
  • Retail packaging and barcodes are applied at each workshop before consolidation, so the assortment does not need re-labelling on arrival.
  • Load, stability and hinge-cycle checks are run on the assembled furniture rather than on the frame alone.

Buyer checklist

  • State the full SKU mix at RFQ; consolidation only saves money when the assortment is known up front.
  • Ask which parts need a test report (load-bearing frames, food-contact liners, lithium lamps) and nominate the lab early.
  • Require one carton manifest and one invoice for the whole assortment, not one set per workshop.

QC & inspection focus

  • Assembled furniture units are load- and stability-checked with hinge-cycle sampling from the pilot batch.
  • Carton counts, barcodes and retail inserts are cross-checked against the consolidated manifest before sealing.

500 pcs per item, with mixed-SKU consolidation allowed. That lets you test several products in one shipment instead of committing to a full container of a single SKU.

Can you combine furniture, lighting and soft goods in one order?

Yes. We coordinate the different workshops, then consolidate the assortment into one container with a single commercial invoice, packing list and carton manifest.

Yes. Mixed-SKU consolidation is allowed at 500 pcs per item, so a new line can be tested across several products in one container with one export document set.

Do you certify the load rating of a camping chair?
