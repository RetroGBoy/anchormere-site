FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What is the difference between a sourcing agent and a factory?

A factory makes one product line; an agent coordinates many vetted factories, quotes like-for-like, runs QC and consolidates shipments. You get one accountable partner instead of many suppliers.

How does an agent protect the buyer?

By vetting suppliers, holding a single quality baseline, catching defects before shipment, and handling contracts, Incoterms and export docs on your behalf.

What are typical agent fees?

Structures vary — fixed commission, markup or retainer. We quote transparently per RFQ so you can compare against buying direct.

Can you consolidate orders from multiple factories?

Yes. We merge scattered SKUs into one shipment with a single export document package to cut freight and clearance friction.

How do I verify the agent is real?

Request the operating entity (Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.), business license, a recorded shop-floor video and references — we provide all on request.
