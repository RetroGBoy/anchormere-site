Frequently asked about rack accessories

机柜配件常见问答

What rack accessories do you supply?
Blanking panels, cage nuts and screws, fixed and sliding shelves, mounting brackets, vented panels and cable management arms — standard 19-inch fitment for server racks and network cabinets.
What is your minimum order quantity?
500 pieces per item for standard accessories. Mixed-SKU consolidation is available to reach the MOQ across a single shipment.
Can you build to print for custom rack parts?
Yes. Send a drawing or sample; we quote build-to-print with tooling cost carried by the buyer where applicable, routed to a vetted Greater Bay Area workshop.
