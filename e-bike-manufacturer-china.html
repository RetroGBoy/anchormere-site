Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

Electric two-wheelers became one of China's fastest-growing export categories in 2026, with first-quarter volumes up sharply and strong demand from emerging markets seeking low-cost mobility as well as from European dealers replacing short-trip car use. For an importer the difficulty is not price, it is configuration: voltage standards, motor rating, pedal-assist rules, brake and lighting requirements, battery documentation and charger plugs all differ by destination, and a container built to the wrong spec is expensive to rework after arrival. Anchormere coordinates the physical build in the Greater Bay Area and the wider two-wheeler cluster: frame and fork, hub or mid-drive motor, controller and display, battery pack and charger, wiring harness, brakes and lighting, and final assembly and functional test. We quote against a written configuration sheet that states the destination market, voltage, motor rating and required documentation, and we adapt the build before production rather than after. We do not issue type approval, homologation or battery certifications; those are obtained by the importer with its nominated body. Our role is a consistent, traceable build with a full export document package and one accountable coordinator.

Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

An e-bike container is usually wrong because of configuration, not workmanship: a charger plug, assist limit or lighting spec that fits one market makes the whole shipment hard to sell in another.

Process & technical notes

  • A configuration sheet naming destination, voltage, motor rating, assist behaviour and plug type is signed before any production slot is booked.
  • Battery packs, chargers and controllers are matched as a set and verified together on the assembled bike.
  • End-of-line lighting, brake and charge checks are run against the destination spec, not a generic checklist.

Buyer checklist

  • State the destination market and its assist, power and lighting rules at RFQ; they determine the bill of materials.
  • Confirm who obtains type approval and battery certification before tooling, not at booking.
  • Ask for the signed configuration sheet to accompany the commercial invoice so the receiver can reconcile spec to goods.

QC & inspection focus

  • Every unit is checked for drive, brake, lighting and charging, with results filed per batch.
  • Battery pack serials are matched to the bike frame number and included in the shipment traceability sheet.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you handle type approval or homologation?

No. The importer obtains type approval, homologation and battery certifications with its nominated body. We build to a signed configuration sheet and supply the technical and traceability data those applications need.

What is the MOQ for an e-bike model?

500 pcs for a pilot batch on an existing frame platform. A new frame or a new motor platform needs a longer sample phase, quoted separately.

Can you adapt the bike to my market's rules?

Yes, before production. The configuration sheet states destination, voltage, motor rating, pedal-assist behaviour, lighting, brake spec and charger plug, and the build is adapted to it rather than reworked after arrival.

Which components do you integrate?

Frame and fork, hub or mid-drive motor, controller and display, battery pack and charger, harness, brakes, lighting and sensors, plus final assembly and end-of-line functional test.

How do you verify a batch before shipment?

Each unit runs drive, brake, lighting and charge checks; range is sampled from the pilot batch; battery packs are lot-traceable with the record shipped alongside the documents.

Can you supply a bike that is already approved for my market?

We do not issue or claim type approval. We build to the configuration sheet you supply and provide the technical data your nominated approval body requires; the approval itself is obtained by the importer.

Why is a configuration sheet mandatory?

Because voltage, motor rating, assist limits, lighting and charger plugs are market-specific. Without a signed sheet, a batch can be built correctly to the wrong spec and become expensive to rework after arrival.
