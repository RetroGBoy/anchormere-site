Product line · Smart Toys & Devices

Smart toys & connected devices, built in the world's toy + electronics belt

智能玩具与联网设备，全球玩具电子带的整合直供

Shenzhen and Dongguan form the world's largest toy-and-electronics belt — where a plush toy, a screen module and a wireless chip converge on one line. We integrate smart toys, educational robots and connected devices: enclosures, PCBA and final assembly, with the electronics sourced across the cluster. What we supply

供应清单

Smart toys

Screen-based, connected and voice toys — the electronics, the plastic/plush shell and the assembly, integrated under one accountable partner.

Educational robots

Programmable kits, robot companions and STEM devices — structure, PCBA and box-build for the education market.

Connected devices & IoT gadgets

Wi-Fi/Bluetooth modules, sensors and small connected hardware — from concept to small-batch production.

Enclosures & housings

Injection-molded, overmolded and soft-touch housings for toys and devices — ABS/PC, silicone and plush-friendly frames.

PCBA & box-build

Prototype-to-volume PCBA, SMT and full box-build. We run the build; firmware and circuit design stay with your team or a specialist partner.

Plush + electronic hybrids

Frequently asked about smart toys

智能玩具常见问答

Do you make complete smart toys?
Yes — screen toys, connected toys and voice toys, including the electronics, the shell and final assembly. We integrate; we don't own the toy's firmware or content, which stay with your team.
How fast can you prototype?
Very. In Shenzhen's Huaqiangbei you can pull together a working prototype — modules, shell and assembly — within about a week, then iterate fast on low volumes.
What is your minimum order quantity?
500 pieces per item for standard parts; finished-device runs are quoted per project. Mixed-SKU consolidation helps reach the MOQ in a single shipment.
