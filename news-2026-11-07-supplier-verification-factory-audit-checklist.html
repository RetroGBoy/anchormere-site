Supplier verification in China: a ten-point factory audit checklist

中国供应商验厂：十项工厂审核清单

A trading company can produce a convincing website, a borrowed certificate and a photo of somebody else's line. Verification is a document-and-evidence exercise, not a video call. Here are ten checks we run before a Greater Bay Area supplier enters a programme.

贸易公司可以做出漂亮网站、借来的证书和别人产线的照片。验厂是「文件加证据」的核查，不是一次视频通话。以下是大湾区供应商进入项目前我们执行的十项检查。

The ten checks

01. Verify the legal entity, not the brand核验法律主体而非品牌名

Check the business licence, registered address and legal representative against the entity that will sign the contract and receive payment.

核对营业执照、注册地址与法定代表人，确认与签约主体、收款主体一致。

02. Separate factory from trading intermediary区分工厂与贸易中介

Ask for the production address, utility records and equipment list; a factory audit should take place where the goods are actually made.

索取生产地址、水电记录与设备清单；验厂应在实际生产地而非办公室进行。

03. Assess real capacity against your forecast用你的预测校验真实产能

Compare stated monthly output with line count, shift pattern and current order book; a supplier running at full capacity cannot absorb a rush order.

把声称月产能与产线数、班次、在手订单比对；满产供应商接不了急单。

04. Audit the quality management system审核质量管理体系

A working QMS shows up in document control, incoming inspection, non-conformance handling and calibration records — not just a framed certificate.

有效的 QMS 体现在文件管控、来料检验、不合格品处理与校准记录上，而不只是一张挂墙证书。

05. Check social compliance separately单独核查社会责任合规

For retail-bound programmes, amfori BSCI or a SMETA report covers labour, health and safety, environment and ethics on a shared audit format.

面向零售渠道的项目，amfori BSCI 或 SMETA 报告以共享审核格式覆盖劳工、健康安全、环境与道德。

06. Validate every certificate by file number按档案号逐一验证证书

Confirm scope, model list and validity directly with the issuing scheme — IECEE and UL Solutions both maintain verifiable certification records.

直接向发证体系核对覆盖范围、型号清单与有效期；IECEE 与 UL Solutions 均提供可验证的认证记录。

07. Require a first article inspection要求首件检验

A first article inspection measures the first unit against every drawing dimension before the batch proceeds, catching setup errors early.

首件检验在批次继续前按图纸全尺寸测量首件，及早发现调机错误。

08. Test the corrective-action loop测试纠正措施闭环

Ask for a recent non-conformance and the CAPA record behind it; a factory with no recorded defects usually has no recording system.

索取最近一次不合格记录及其 CAPA 档案；「零缺陷记录」往往意味着根本没有记录体系。

09. Demand batch traceability要求批次可追溯

Batch traceability must link a finished unit back to material lots, machine, operator and test result within a workable time window.

批次可追溯须能在合理时间内把成品回溯到物料批、机台、操作员与测试结果。

10. Use remote verification as a supplement把远程验厂作为补充手段

Recorded shop-floor video, by-process photo galleries, a document wall and live inspection by appointment narrow the gap between visits — but do not replace an on-site audit for high-risk programmes.

按工序录像、车间影像集、单证墙与预约实时验视可缩小两次到厂之间的空档，但高风险项目仍不能替代现场审核。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

Factory audit

An on-site or remote assessment of a supplier's capability, capacity, quality system and social compliance before awarding a PO.

下单前对供应商能力、产能、质量体系与社会责任做的现场或远程评估。

QMS

Quality Management System: the documented process set (commonly audited against ISO 9001) controlling how a factory builds and releases product.

质量管理体系，即控制工厂生产与放行流程的成套文件化流程。

SMETA

Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit: a widely used shared audit format covering labour, health and safety, environment and business ethics.

常用的共享社会责任审核框架，覆盖劳工、健康安全、环境与商业道德。

amfori BSCI

A supply-chain social compliance system that sets a code of conduct and audit methodology for buyer-appointed factories.

供应链社会责任体系，为买方指定工厂提供行为准则与审核方法。

CAPA

Corrective and Preventive Action: the documented root-cause and closure process a factory must run after a defect is found.

纠正与预防措施：发现缺陷后的根因分析与闭环整改流程。

First article inspection

A documented measurement of the first unit off a production run against every drawing dimension before the batch continues.

对首件按图纸全尺寸测量并记录，合格后才继续生产。

Batch traceability

The ability to link a finished unit back to its material lots, machine, operator and test record via a lot or serial code.

通过批次或序列号追溯到物料批、机台、操作员与测试记录的能力。

Golden sample

The sealed, signed reference unit that both parties use as the acceptance baseline for mass production.

双方封样签认的基准样机，作为量产验收依据。

First-pass yield

The share of units that pass all tests without rework on the first run; a direct indicator of process maturity.

一次通过全部测试、无需返修的比例，直接反映工艺成熟度。

MOQ

Minimum order quantity: the smallest volume a factory will run, driven by material lot sizes and line changeover cost.

工厂可承接的最小订单量，由物料批量与换线成本决定。
