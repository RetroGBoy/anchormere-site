How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Amazon private label adds a fulfilment layer to manufacturing: FNSKU labelling, carton rules, hazmat review and listing compliance must be ready before the goods leave the floor.

What we do, step by step

  • FNSKU or manufacturer barcode labelling is applied at unit level and verified by scan before carton sealing.
  • Carton weight, dimensions and the shipment-plan ID are checked against the FBA inbound rules for the destination FC.
  • Battery, magnet, liquid or powder goods go through a hazmat / documentation review before booking.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • State the marketplace, fulfilment channel and whether you need FBA prep or retail-ready packaging at RFQ.
  • Provide the FNSKU file and carton-label format before the production slot, not after packing.
  • Check category-specific compliance (CPSIA for children's items, FCC/CE for radio, UN38.3 for batteries).

How we verify & report

  • 100% barcode scan on units and cartons, plus a carton drop test on packed masters.
  • Carton content manifest cross-checked against the shipment plan before the container is sealed.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can you produce FNSKU-ready cartons and polybag warnings?

Yes. We apply FNSKU labels, Made-in-China marks, bilingual carton labels and polybag suffocation warnings before shipment so goods arrive FBA-ready.

What is the MOQ for Amazon private label?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation lets you split one shipment across several SKUs to lower per-SKU risk during test marketing.

Do you help with bundles, inserts and retail packaging?

Yes. We handle bundling, kitting, branded inserts, retail boxes and mailer cartons so the products are shelf- and ship-ready.

Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?

We prepare FBA-forwarding documents (CI, PL, FNSKU mapping) and coordinate with your freight forwarder. We do not act as the importer of record.

How fast can I get a sample and start production?

Samples in 7-12 days on existing tooling, 15-25 days for new tooling; production starts 20-35 days after sample approval.

Can you apply FNSKU labels for my shipment?

Yes. Send the FNSKU file and we apply and scan-verify labels at unit level, then consolidate multi-SKU cartons into one export document package.

Do you handle battery products for FBA?

Yes, subject to UN38.3 and the carrier's hazmat documentation; we flag battery SKUs at RFQ so the test report and packing method are ready before booking.
