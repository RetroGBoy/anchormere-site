Why build it in the Greater Bay Area

为什么在大湾区做

In 2026 a factory quote is only the first line of the bill. The United States suspended its USD 800 de minimis exemption for commercial shipments and the European Union removed its EUR 150 low-value duty exemption, so low-value parcels no longer clear duty-free and every order needs a formal or informal entry. Buyers who still price from the FOB number alone routinely discover that duty, freight, entry fees and brokerage add a substantial layer before the goods reach a shelf. Anchormere works on the export side of that equation: we state the Incoterm up front, break out packaging, QC, inland freight and consolidation as separate lines, and issue a clean commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin so your broker can classify and enter the shipment without chasing ten suppliers. We are not a customs broker, we do not act as importer of record, and we do not publish binding duty rates; those come from your licensed broker and the official tariff schedule in force on the day of entry. What we do is make every cost line visible before you wire a deposit, so the margin you modelled is the margin you keep. Confirm current official texts and rates at RFQ.

How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Landed cost is an allocation problem as much as a price problem: shared freight, duty and fees must be split by whatever actually drives them, or a mixed-SKU shipment quietly misprices half the carton.

What we do, step by step

  • Each cost line is quoted separately (product, tooling, packaging, QC, inland freight, consolidation) so duty is never applied to a bundled number.
  • Freight is allocated by the real constraint — weight for dense goods, volume for bulky light goods — rather than divided evenly across units.
  • Carton manifest, invoice value and packing declaration are cross-checked before the container is sealed, so the broker sees one consistent set of figures.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • State the destination market and the Incoterm at RFQ; the duty base and who pays the entry fees change with both.
  • Ask your broker to confirm the HS classification before the production slot, not after the goods are packed.
  • Budget a buffer for freight and duty volatility; an old freight estimate is the most common reason a 'cheap' order loses money.

How we verify & report

  • Commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin are checked against each other for quantity, value and description consistency.
  • A pre-shipment photo set and carton manifest are issued so the receiver can reconcile the shipment without opening every carton.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What exactly is included in landed cost?

The product cost, tooling and packaging, QC, international freight and insurance, import duty and tariffs, customs entry fees (MPF, HMF where applicable), brokerage, drayage and inbound receiving. We make the export-side lines explicit in the quote.

Does the USD 800 de minimis exemption still apply?

No for commercial shipments: the US suspended it and the EU removed its low-value duty exemption in 2026. Low-value parcels now require an entry and applicable duty. Confirm the current rule text with your broker at RFQ.

Can Anchormere tell me my duty rate?

We do not issue binding classification or duty rates. We supply the product description, materials, BOM and use data your licensed broker needs to classify the goods against the official tariff schedule.

Which Incoterm should I choose for a first order?

It depends on who controls freight and who bears the import side. We quote EXW, FOB Shenzhen, CIF or DDP so you can compare like-for-like; the choice is yours and should be stated before production.

How do I avoid a surprise cost between quote and shipment?

Ask for a fresh freight quote on actual packed dimensions right before booking, keep all cost lines separate, and hold a buffer for freight and duty volatility. Our 48h quote lists every export-side line up front.

Do you include duty in your quote?

No. Our quote covers the export-side lines and states the Incoterm; duty, entry fees and brokerage are buyer-side and depend on the official tariff schedule in force at entry. We give your broker the product data needed to calculate them.

Can you ship DDP so I get one all-in number?

DDP can be quoted, but the duty and clearance component still rests on a licensed broker's classification and current rates. We separate that line so you can see what is a supplier cost and what is a statutory charge.
