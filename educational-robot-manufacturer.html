Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

Educational robots are a kit-consistency problem: servo torque, sensor calibration and the coding curriculum must match a single BOM across every classroom set.

Process & technical notes

  • Servo gear trains, hall or IR sensors and the main controller are locked to one BOM so lesson code behaves identically across a class set.
  • Snap-fit structural parts are validated for repeated assembly cycles, since students rebuild the same kit dozens of times.
  • Firmware exposes a fixed block-based API; a late API change breaks the printed curriculum and the teacher guide.

Buyer checklist

  • Confirm age grading, small-parts limits and EN71 / ASTM F963 / CPSIA scope before design freeze.
  • Specify whether curriculum, teacher guide and app are in scope — hardware and content are separate deliverables.
  • Ask for spare-part ratios for servos and cables; classroom wear is far higher than consumer use.

QC & inspection focus

  • Servo stall-torque and no-load current sampled per lot, plus 500-cycle snap-fit assembly test on structural parts.
  • Every kit is power-on tested with a scripted lesson sequence before packing; missing-part check by weight.
