Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A TWS program is won or lost on acoustic tuning and battery fit — the driver, Bluetooth SoC and charging-case cell must be qualified as one system, not sourced separately.

Process & technical notes

  • Bluetooth SoC selection (BES, Qualcomm, Realtek) fixes the ANC, LE Audio and pairing ceiling before any tooling starts.
  • Driver and acoustic chamber tuning is validated on prototype samples; a 0.2 mm change in enclosure wall thickness shifts frequency response by several dB.
  • Charging-case cell, pogo-pin contact and hinge tolerance are gated together to prevent intermittent-charging returns.

Buyer checklist

  • State ANC / ENC and LE Audio requirements at RFQ stage — they change the SoC BOM and the certification path.
  • Approve a sealed golden sample carrying your own logo before tooling release; laser-marked logos cannot be reworked.
  • Budget for Bluetooth SIG qualification, CE / FCC / RoHS and UN38.3 battery transport testing.

QC & inspection focus

  • 100% RF pairing test and audio frequency sweep, with ANC depth sampled per production lot.
  • Battery cycle test and 1.2 m drop test on the charging case; pogo-pin contact resistance logged per lot.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Can I customize my own brand logo on the earbuds?

Yes. We support private label with your logo on the enclosure, packaging and user manual; MOQ starts at 500 pcs per SKU.

What is the MOQ for private-label earbuds?

500 pcs per SKU, with mixed-SKU consolidation allowed across one shipment.

Do you provide samples before mass production?

Yes. We ship pre-production samples (paid, refundable against the bulk order) for your approval before tooling and the mass run.

Which Bluetooth chips do you use?

BES, Qualcomm and Realtek platforms; we recommend per your budget and feature set (ANC, ENC, low latency).

Can I specify my own Bluetooth SoC or companion app?

Yes for ODM programs. For private-label runs we normally qualify a proven SoC so SIG and radio certification stay on a short lead time.

How long does private-label earbud tooling take?

Injection tooling is typically 25–35 days, followed by 30–45 days for pilot run and mass production at 500 pcs per SKU MOQ.
