Engineering & sourcing notes

工程与采购要点

A TWS program is won or lost on acoustic tuning and battery fit — the driver, Bluetooth SoC and charging-case cell must be qualified as one system, not sourced separately.

Process & technical notes

  • Bluetooth SoC selection (BES, Qualcomm, Realtek) fixes the ANC, LE Audio and pairing ceiling before any tooling starts.
  • Driver and acoustic chamber tuning is validated on prototype samples; a 0.2 mm change in enclosure wall thickness shifts frequency response by several dB.
  • Charging-case cell, pogo-pin contact and hinge tolerance are gated together to prevent intermittent-charging returns.

Buyer checklist

  • State ANC / ENC and LE Audio requirements at RFQ stage — they change the SoC BOM and the certification path.
  • Approve a sealed golden sample carrying your own logo before tooling release; laser-marked logos cannot be reworked.
  • Budget for Bluetooth SIG qualification, CE / FCC / RoHS and UN38.3 battery transport testing.

QC & inspection focus

  • 100% RF pairing test and audio frequency sweep, with ANC depth sampled per production lot.
  • Battery cycle test and 1.2 m drop test on the charging case; pogo-pin contact resistance logged per lot.

Acoustic, RF and battery decisions in a TWS program

TWS 项目里的声学、射频与电池决策

These notes are compiled per category from public engineering standards and the failure modes buyers most often meet. Figures are typical industry ranges; the binding version is the one confirmed against your drawing, target market and RFQ.

  • Driver impedance and sensitivity set the acoustic ceiling before any DSP tuning; a 10 mm dynamic driver and a balanced armature need different chamber volumes and cannot share a housing.
  • Bluetooth SoC selection (BES, Qualcomm, Realtek) fixes ANC depth, LE Audio support and multipoint pairing; changing it after tooling forces a full RF re-qualification.
  • Pogo-pin contact resistance in the charging case is the top field-return cause; specify gold-plating thickness and a wipe-cycle target rather than accepting a generic contact spec.
  • Battery cell, protection PCM and charging-case firmware must be validated as one system under IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3, with the pack-level test report matching the exact cell model in the BOM.

A real trade-off at RFQ stage

A buyer asks for 30 dB ANC and a 6 mm slim case in the same RFQ. Physics pushes back: the feedforward mic needs spacing from the driver, and a smaller case halves cell capacity. We normally present two BOMs at RFQ — an acoustic-first build and a runtime-first build — so the trade-off is decided before tooling, not after the first pilot run.
