How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Contract electronics manufacturing is a traceability contract: turnkey versus consigned material, DFM sign-off and lot-level genealogy define who owns a field failure.

What we do, step by step

  • DFM/DFT review is issued before the build, flagging panelisation, fiducials, test-point access and moisture-sensitive parts.
  • Material is either turnkey with documented alternates or consigned with lot-code reconciliation at shipment.
  • Each board carries a serial number linking solder paste lot, reflow profile, operator and test results.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Specify turnkey or consigned, the accepted alternate policy and liability for EOL parts in the RFQ.
  • Provide an ICT/FCT test specification and define the rework limit per board.
  • Ask for the traceability report format and retention period before the pilot run.

How we verify & report

  • SPI + AOI + X-ray + ICT/FCT with per-serial test logs; yield Pareto issued per lot.
  • MSL component baking and floor-life tracking, plus ESD audit records on the line.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

Do you offer turnkey (consignment + assembly) PCBA?

Yes. We do BOM sourcing, SMT/DIP, testing and box-build as a one-stop contract.

Can you start from a single prototype board?

Yes. Many shops support 1-piece SMT prototypes with 24–48h turnaround.

How do you ensure quality?

Authorised-distributor sourcing, incoming inspection and AOI/X-ray/ICT test with full traceability.

What files do you need to quote?

Gerber, BOM, centroid/placement file and assembly drawing; quote within 48h.

What is the difference between turnkey and consigned in your RFQ?

Turnkey means we source and warrant the BOM against agreed alternates; consigned means you supply parts and we warrant the assembly process and reconcile your stock by lot code.

Do you keep test records after shipment?

Yes. Per-serial test logs, reflow profile and material lot codes are retained against the project code and can be produced for a field-failure investigation.
