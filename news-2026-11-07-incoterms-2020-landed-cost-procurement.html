International trade procurement: Incoterms 2020 and landed cost, point by point

国际贸易采购：逐条拆解 Incoterms 2020 与到岸成本

Two buyers can receive the same ex-works price and end up with very different real costs. The difference sits in the trade term, the duty line and the document chain. Here are ten points we use to turn a factory quote into a defensible landed cost, with the official references attached.

两个买家拿到同样的出厂价，最终真实成本可能相差很大，差异就在贸易术语、税则行与单证链。以下十点用于把工厂报价还原成可核对的到岸成本，并附官方出处。

The ten checks

01. Name the rule and the named place写明术语与指定地点

The ICC Incoterms 2020 rules only work when the term is followed by a named place — FOB Shenzhen, not just FOB. Eleven rules exist; four are for sea and inland waterway only.

ICC Incoterms 2020 术语必须带指定地点才有效——应写 FOB 深圳，而非只写 FOB。共 11 个术语，其中 4 个仅适用海运与内河运输。

02. Know where risk transfers明确风险转移点

Under FOB risk passes once goods are on board; under EXW it passes at the factory gate; under DAP only on arrival ready for unloading. Insurance should follow that boundary.

FOB 风险在装船后转移，EXW 在工厂门口转移，DAP 到指定地点待卸货时转移；保险安排应对齐该分界。

03. Separate who clears export and import分清出口与进口清关责任

EXW leaves export formalities to the buyer, while DDP puts import clearance and duties on the seller. Most GBA factories are not set up to deliver DDP into your market.

EXW 由买方办理出口手续，DDP 则由卖方承担进口清关与税费；多数大湾区工厂并不具备向你的市场做 DDP 的能力。

04. Build the duty line from the HS code从 HS 编码推导税则行

Start with the WCO Harmonized System, then read the national schedule — the U.S. HTS or the EU tariff — and cross-check bound rates via the WTO.

先依 WCO 协调制度确定 6 位编码，再查国别税则（美国 HTS 或欧盟税则），并用 WTO 数据核对约束税率。

05. Add every non-tariff cost补齐非关税成本

A landed-cost model should include freight, insurance, terminal handling, customs brokerage, VAT or GST, and compliance testing — not only the duty percentage.

到岸成本模型应含运保费、码头操作费、报关代理费、增值税/GST 与合规检测费，而不只是关税税率。

06. Verify origin, not just the shipping route核验原产地而非只看运输路线

The WTO rules of origin material and the destination authority decide eligibility for preferential rates; transhipment does not by itself confer origin.

WTO 原产地规则及目的国主管机构决定优惠税率资格；单纯转运并不产生原产地资格。

07. Use trade data to sanity-check volume用贸易数据校验规模合理性

UN Comtrade and UN statistics let you compare a supplier's claimed export volume with reported bilateral flows before committing to a large PO.

UN Comtrade 与 联合国统计数据库可用于在大额下单前，比对供应商声称的出口规模与公开双边流量。

08. Match documents to the payment term让单证与付款条件匹配

A bill of lading controls title under many payment structures; the ITA documentation guidance sets out which documents a bank or broker will expect.

在多种付款结构下 提单代表货权；ITA 单证指引列明银行或报关行会要求哪些文件。

09. Check market-specific import rules核查目的市场进口规则

The ITA Country Commercial Guides summaries cover local labelling, standards and customs practice that a generic Incoterms clause never addresses.

ITA 国别商业指南涵盖本地标签、标准与通关惯例，这些是通用贸易术语条款无法覆盖的。

10. Model the cash cycle, not only the unit price测算现金周期而非仅看单价

Deposit, balance-before-shipment and in-transit days all carry a financing cost; export credit agencies and the World Bank trade indicators are useful benchmarks for that exposure.

定金、发货前尾款与在途天数都产生资金成本；出口信贷机构与 世界银行贸易指标可作为该敞口的参照。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

Incoterms

The ICC's published trade terms (current edition: Incoterms 2020) that allocate delivery, risk, cost and customs responsibility between seller and buyer.

国际商会发布的贸易术语（现行为 2020 版），划分买卖双方交货、风险、费用与清关责任。

FOB (Free On Board)

Seller clears the goods for export and loads them on the vessel at the named port; risk transfers once on board.

卖方完成出口清关并将货物装上指定港口船舶，风险在装船后转移。

EXW (Ex Works)

Seller only makes goods available at its premises; the buyer carries all transport, export and import formalities.

卖方仅在己方场所交货，运输及进出口手续全部由买方承担。

DAP (Delivered at Place)

Seller delivers to a named place, ready for unloading; the buyer handles import clearance and duties.

卖方运至指定地点（不含卸货），进口清关与税费由买方承担。

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

Seller delivers to the named place with import clearance and duties paid — maximum seller obligation.

卖方承担进口清关与税费并送达指定地点，卖方义务最大。

Landed cost

The full unit cost at the buyer's door: ex-works price, freight, insurance, duties, taxes, handling and compliance spend.

货物到达买方仓库的全部单位成本：出厂价、运保费、关税、税费、杂费与合规支出。

HS code

A six-digit Harmonized System classification assigned by the World Customs Organization; countries append their own digits for tariff and statistical lines.

世界海关组织制定的 6 位商品编码，各国再延长本国税则子目。

Bill of lading

The carrier-issued document that evidences the contract of carriage, receipt of goods and (for an order B/L) title to the cargo.

承运人签发的运输单证，证明运输合同与收货，指示提单还代表货权。

Rules of origin

The criteria that decide the economic nationality of a good and therefore its tariff treatment and eligibility for preferential rates.

判定货物经济国籍的标准，决定适用税率与优惠资格。

Ad valorem duty

A duty calculated as a percentage of the customs value rather than a fixed amount per unit.

按完税价格百分比计征的关税，而非按件固定金额。

Customs valuation

The method customs uses to assign a dutiable value to imported goods, normally based on the transaction value.

海关确定完税价格的方法，通常以成交价格为基础。
