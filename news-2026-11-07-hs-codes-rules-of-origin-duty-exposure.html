HS codes, rules of origin and duty exposure: ten checks for importers

HS 编码、原产地规则与税负：进口方十项核查

Classification errors are the most expensive typo in importing: they compound across every shipment and can trigger retroactive assessments. These ten checks turn a guessed HS code into a defensible one, using the primary sources customs authorities themselves cite.

归类错误是进口中最昂贵的笔误：它会在每一票货上累积，并可能引发追征。以下十项核查把「猜出来的 HS 编码」变成可举证的归类，依据正是海关自身引用的原始来源。

The ten checks

01. Start with the six-digit HS, not a supplier code从 6 位 HS 出发，不采信供应商编码

The WCO Harmonized System defines the global six digits; anything beyond that is a national extension and must be checked in the destination schedule.

WCO 协调制度定义全球通用 6 位；其后的位数是国别延伸，必须在目的国税则中核对。

02. Read the full national tariff line读完整的国别税则行

The U.S. HTS adds statistical suffixes and Chapter 99 measures; EU importers use the integrated tariff via the Taxation & Customs Union.

美国 HTS 附加统计后缀与第 99 章措施；欧盟进口方通过 税务与海关总司使用综合税则。

03. Cross-check bound and applied rates交叉核对约束税率与实施税率

The WTO tariff data distinguishes bound commitments from applied rates — the number that actually lands on your entry.

WTO 关税数据区分约束承诺与实施税率，后者才是真正落在报关单上的数字。

04. Document the classification rationale留存归类依据

Record why a heading was chosen (material, function, assembly state) and keep the General Rules of Interpretation reasoning in the file for audit.

记录选择某税目的理由（材质、功能、装配状态），并把归类总规则的推理留档备查。

05. Apply rules of origin correctly正确适用原产地规则

The WTO rules of origin material explains how substantial transformation and tariff-shift tests determine economic nationality.

WTO 原产地规则资料说明实质性改变与税则归类改变测试如何判定经济国籍。

06. Substantiate preferential claims为优惠税率提供证据

A certificate of origin or approved origin declaration is what makes a preferential rate usable; a supplier's assertion is not.

原产地证书或合规的原产地声明才是优惠税率可用的依据，供应商口头说明不算。

07. Check customs valuation inputs核查完税价格构成

Customs valuation normally starts from transaction value but adds assists, tooling, royalties and certain freight elements — all of which affect the duty base.

完税价格通常以成交价为基础，但需加入协助、模具、特许权费与部分运费，都会影响计税基数。

08. Distinguish ad valorem from specific duties区分从价税与从量税

An ad valorem duty scales with value; specific and compound duties behave differently and change the sensitivity of your landed-cost model.

从价税随货值变动，从量税与复合税逻辑不同，会改变到岸成本模型的敏感度。

09. Screen for trade-remedy measures排查贸易救济措施

Anti-dumping, countervailing and safeguard measures apply by product and origin; the Country Commercial Guides and ITA resources flag where to check.

反倾销、反补贴与保障措施按产品与原产地适用；国别商业指南与 ITA 资料提示查询路径。

10. Reconcile with reported trade flows与公开贸易流量对账

UN Comtrade and World Bank trade indicators help confirm that a claimed product-origin mix is plausible before scaling volume.

UN Comtrade 与 世界银行贸易指标可在放量前核验所声称的产品-原产地组合是否合理。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

HS code

A six-digit Harmonized System classification assigned by the World Customs Organization; countries append their own digits for tariff and statistical lines.

世界海关组织制定的 6 位商品编码，各国再延长本国税则子目。

HTS

The Harmonized Tariff Schedule: the destination-country tariff schedule built on the HS, used to look up duty rates and statistical suffixes.

基于 HS 的目的国关税表，用于查询税率与统计后缀。

Rules of origin

The criteria that decide the economic nationality of a good and therefore its tariff treatment and eligibility for preferential rates.

判定货物经济国籍的标准，决定适用税率与优惠资格。

Certificate of origin

A document attesting where goods were produced, required for preferential tariffs and by some customs authorities.

证明货物原产地的文件，优惠税率与部分海关要求提供。

Customs valuation

The method customs uses to assign a dutiable value to imported goods, normally based on the transaction value.

海关确定完税价格的方法，通常以成交价格为基础。

Ad valorem duty

A duty calculated as a percentage of the customs value rather than a fixed amount per unit.

按完税价格百分比计征的关税，而非按件固定金额。

Landed cost

The full unit cost at the buyer's door: ex-works price, freight, insurance, duties, taxes, handling and compliance spend.

货物到达买方仓库的全部单位成本：出厂价、运保费、关税、税费、杂费与合规支出。

Bill of lading

The carrier-issued document that evidences the contract of carriage, receipt of goods and (for an order B/L) title to the cargo.

承运人签发的运输单证，证明运输合同与收货，指示提单还代表货权。

De minimis threshold

The shipment value below which a destination market may waive duties or simplified entry — rules vary by market and change often.

目的市场免征关税或简化申报的货值门槛，各市场不同且常调整。

Incoterms

The ICC's published trade terms (current edition: Incoterms 2020) that allocate delivery, risk, cost and customs responsibility between seller and buyer.

国际商会发布的贸易术语（现行为 2020 版），划分买卖双方交货、风险、费用与清关责任。
