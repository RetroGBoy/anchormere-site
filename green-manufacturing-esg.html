Green manufacturing & ESG: compliance buyers should ask for

绿色制造与 ESG：买家应索取的合规项

Green trade rules are tightening worldwide — substance restrictions, carbon disclosure and supply-chain due diligence are now part of doing business. When sourcing from the Greater Bay Area, the right documents and audits protect you at the border and with your own customers.

The compliance checklist

RoHS / REACHRestriction of hazardous substances and chemical registration for EU/UK-bound goods.
Material declarationsBoM-level substance data (e.g., IPC/1752-style) for electronics.
Carbon & energyFactory energy data and, where required, product carbon footprint for CBAM-exposed goods.
Social auditsISO 45001 / SA8000-style or customer-required labor and safety audits.
TraceabilityLot-level records supporting recalls and due diligence.

Why it matters now

  • Border risk — missing substance declarations can stall clearance.
  • Customer demand — retailers and platforms increasingly require ESG evidence upstream.
  • Reputation — documented compliance de-risks your own brand.
