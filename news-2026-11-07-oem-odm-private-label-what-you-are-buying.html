OEM, ODM or private label: ten things buyers are actually signing

OEM、ODM 还是贴牌：买家实际签下的十件事

Buyers often use OEM, ODM and private label interchangeably. The factory does not — the three models allocate design authority, tooling ownership and liability very differently. These ten points describe what you are actually committing to before the first purchase order.

买家常把 OEM、ODM 与贴牌混为一谈，工厂却不会：三种模式对设计权、模具所有权与责任归属的划分完全不同。以下十点说明首张订单之前，你实际承诺了什么。

The ten checks

01. Write down which model the quote covers在报价中写明所属模式

OEM means production to your released drawing; ODM starts from the factory's base design; private label is a standard product with your brand and packaging.

OEM 按你发布的图纸生产；ODM 以工厂基础设计为起点；贴牌则是标准品换品牌与包装。

02. State who owns the design明确设计归属

In a true build-to-print engagement the factory has no design-change authority; any engineering change needs a written revision and a new sign-off.

真正的 按图制造合作中，工厂无设计变更权；任何工程变更都需书面改版并重新签认。

03. Settle tooling ownership before payment付款前先谈定模具归属

Tooling is usually buyer-funded but factory-held; the PO should say who owns it, where it is stored, and under what conditions it can be released or moved.

模具通常由买方出资、工厂保管；订单应写明所有权、存放地点以及可移交或转厂的条件。

04. Transfer IP in writing以书面条款转移知识产权

An IP assignment covering drawings, moulds, firmware and artwork is stronger than a purchase order alone, and should survive termination of the relationship.

覆盖图纸、模具、固件与美术作品的 知识产权转让条款比单独一张订单更有效，且应在合作终止后继续有效。

05. Use the right confidentiality instrument使用适配的保密文件

A standard Western NDA is often weak in a China supply chain; an NNN agreement adds non-use and non-circumvention, which are the clauses that actually matter.

普通西式 NDA 在中国供应链中常显不足；NNN 协议补充不使用与不绕开条款，才是真正起作用的部分。

06. Protect the brand before artwork is printed印刷美术稿前先护住品牌

Trade mark filing through the USPTO or the WIPO route should precede packaging production, not follow the first shipment.

通过 USPTO 或 WIPO 途径的商标注册应早于包装生产，而不是首批发货之后。

07. Freeze the BOM and approved alternates冻结 BOM 与认可替代料

A released BOM should name manufacturer and part number for every line and list approved alternates; silent substitution is a common source of field failures.

发布的 BOM 应逐行写明厂商与料号并列出认可替代料；悄悄换料是现场失效的常见原因。

08. Seal a golden sample before mass production量产前封存签认样机

A golden sample plus a signed inspection sheet gives both sides one objective acceptance baseline for colour, finish, fit and function.

签样加双方签署的检验表，为颜色、表面、配合与功能提供唯一客观的验收基准。

09. Allocate conformity and marking duties划分合格评定与标识责任

Decide who produces the Declaration of Conformity and holds test reports; the EU conformity assessment framework and IECEE CB Scheme change what evidence is needed per market.

明确由谁出具符合性声明并持有测试报告；欧盟合格评定框架与 IECEE CB 体系决定各市场所需证据不同。

10. Agree the MOQ and changeover logic约定 MOQ 与换线逻辑

MOQ is driven by material lot sizes and line changeover, not by goodwill; ask which components set the floor and whether mixed-SKU consolidation is allowed.

MOQ 由物料批量与换线成本决定，而非人情；问清由哪些物料决定下限、是否允许混 SKU 拼单。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

OEM

Original Equipment Manufacturer: production to the buyer's own specification and drawing, with the buyer holding the design.

按买方规格与图纸生产，设计归买方所有。

ODM

Original Design Manufacturer: the factory owns a base design that the buyer rebrands or modifies.

工厂拥有基础设计，买方贴牌或做局部修改。

Private label

A factory-made standard product sold under the buyer's brand, usually with custom packaging and artwork.

工厂标准品贴买方品牌销售，通常仅定制包装与外观。

Build-to-print

Manufacture strictly to the buyer's released drawing and BOM, with no factory-side design authority.

严格按买方发布的图纸与 BOM 生产，工厂无设计变更权。

Tooling

The moulds, jigs, fixtures and test rigs built for a specific product; ownership and payment terms should be written into the PO.

为特定产品制作的模具、夹具与测试治具，所有权与付款条件应写入订单。

IP assignment

A written clause transferring ownership of drawings, moulds and firmware deliverables from the factory to the buyer.

将图纸、模具与固件成果所有权从工厂转移给买方的书面条款。

NNN agreement

A China-focused non-disclosure, non-use and non-circumvention contract, distinct from a standard Western NDA.

面向中国供应链的保密、不使用、不绕开协议，区别于普通西式 NDA。

Golden sample

The sealed, signed reference unit that both parties use as the acceptance baseline for mass production.

双方封样签认的基准样机，作为量产验收依据。

BOM

Bill of materials: the complete part list with manufacturer names, part numbers and approved alternates.

完整物料清单，含厂商、料号与认可替代料。

MOQ

Minimum order quantity: the smallest volume a factory will run, driven by material lot sizes and line changeover cost.

工厂可承接的最小订单量，由物料批量与换线成本决定。
