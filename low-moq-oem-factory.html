How the engagement works

合作如何推进

Low-MOQ manufacturing is an economics problem, not a capacity one: shared tooling, a fixed BOM and mixed-SKU consolidation are what make 500 pcs viable.

What we do, step by step

  • Shared or modular tooling keeps the one-time tooling cost off a small first order; only brand-specific inserts are new.
  • A frozen BOM for the first run avoids line re-qualification; alternates are qualified only after the pilot.
  • Mixed-SKU consolidation lets several low-volume items share one shipment and one export document package.

What to prepare as a buyer

  • Prioritise one colourway and one firmware build for the first order; variants multiply changeover cost.
  • Decide whether you want a pilot run with a small engineering batch before the 500 pcs production gate.
  • Ask which components have long lead times so the first order is not held by a single EOL part.

How we verify & report

  • First-piece and last-piece inspection on short runs, since there is no steady-state process to sample.
  • Same AQL and by-process photo documentation as full production runs — volume does not reduce the record.

FAQ

常见问题

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

500 pcs per item. Mixed-SKU consolidation across one shipment is allowed.

How fast is the quote?

Standard items are quoted within 48 hours by an engineer, with lead time and Incoterms stated up front.

How can I verify the factory remotely?

By-process photo gallery, recorded shop-floor video, a certificate & document wall, and live inspection by appointment.

What are the payment terms?

T/T to Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.: 30% deposit to start, 70% before shipment (ship after full payment).

Do you handle finished or powered devices?

Yes. We integrate assembled electronics and PCBA, and consolidate finished devices into one shipment with a full export document package.

What is your minimum order quantity?

500 pcs per SKU; trial orders and mixed-SKU consolidation are supported to reach MOQ.

Can I mix several SKUs in one shipment?

Yes. Mixed-SKU consolidation lets you reach MOQ across one shipment.

Do you support trial / pilot runs?

Yes. Pilot batches help new brands validate before scaling.

How do I negotiate a lower MOQ?

Share your full product roadmap; we consolidate across SKUs and seasons to lower effective MOQ.

Is 500 pcs per item or per order?

500 pcs per item, but mixed-SKU consolidation is allowed, so several 500-pc items can share one order and one shipment.

Can a first order be smaller than 500 pcs?

Engineering prototypes and pilot builds can be smaller; the 500 pcs gate applies to production runs, where line setup and material minimums dominate the unit cost.
