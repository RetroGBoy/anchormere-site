AI data-center hardware sourcing: ten specification points that get missed

AI 数据中心硬件采购：最常被漏掉的十项规格要点

GPU-dense builds change the physical layer: heavier racks, higher cable counts, tighter airflow discipline. The ten points below are the specification items most often left off an RFQ for rack accessories, cabling and installation consumables — and the sources that define them.

GPU 密集型建设改变了物理层：更重的机柜、更高的线缆密度、更严的气流管理。以下十项是机柜配件、布线与安装耗材 RFQ 中最常漏写的规格，以及定义它们的来源。

The ten checks

01. Specify the rack standard and U count写明机柜标准与 U 位数

A 19-inch rack (EIA-310 / IEC 60297) is specified in U increments of 44.45 mm; state total U, usable depth and static load rating.

19 英寸机柜（EIA-310 / IEC 60297）以 U（44.45 毫米）计量；须写明总 U 数、可用深度与静载等级。

02. Set rail and mounting requirements明确导轨与安装要求

Rail type, cage-nut sizing, square-hole vs round-hole uprights and grounding points should be in the drawing, not assumed compatible.

导轨类型、笼式螺母规格、方孔/圆孔立柱与接地点应写入图纸，不能默认兼容。

03. Design cable management around bend radius按弯曲半径设计线缆管理

High-count copper and fibre runs need tray, ladder, J-hook and D-ring sizing that respects minimum bend radius; BICSI publishes the cabling practice.

高芯数铜缆与光纤需托盘、梯架、J 型钩与 D 型环按 最小弯曲半径选型；BICSI 发布布线实践标准。

04. Apply structured cabling administration落实结构化布线管理

Structured cabling needs labelled horizontal and backbone links, patching and records; TIA-942 covers the data-center infrastructure layer.

结构化布线要求水平与主干链路有标签、配线与记录；TIA-942 覆盖数据中心基础设施层。

05. Close unused U-space封堵空余 U 位

Blanking panels stop hot-air recirculation; leaving gaps undermines cooling efficiency regardless of how capable the CRAC units are.

盲板阻止热风回流；无论空调多强，空置 U 位不封堵都会削弱散热效率。

06. Specify power distribution explicitly明确配电规格

PDUs should be specified by input, outlet count, metering, socket type and busbar or feed arrangement — not just total kW.

PDU 须按输入、插座数、计量、插座类型及 母排/馈电方式约定，而不只写总功率。

07. Tie efficiency claims to a metric让能效主张对应指标

PUE is the standard ratio for facility efficiency; ENERGY STAR resources give a common reference for equipment-level expectations.

PUE 是设施能效的标准比值；能源之星资料提供设备级预期的通用参照。

08. Check open hardware specifications核对开放硬件规范

The Open Compute Project publishes open rack, power and server specifications that increasingly shape buyer RFQs.

开放计算项目发布开放的机柜、供电与服务器规范，正越来越多地进入买家 RFQ。

09. Use a recognised facility baseline采用公认的设施基准

Uptime Institute Tier resources are the common language for redundancy and availability discussions with integrators.

Uptime Institute 的 Tier 资料是与集成商讨论冗余与可用性时的通用语言。

10. Plan supply against real demand signals按真实需求信号规划供应

SIA industry data and NIST measurement resources help frame capacity planning; then consolidate multi-SKU hardware into one QC pass and one shipment.

SIA 行业数据与 NIST 计量资料可支撑产能规划；随后把多 SKU 硬件整合为一次质检、一票发货。

Key terms

Domain terms used above, defined against public standards and common trade usage so your RFQ speaks the same language as the factory.

Rack unit (U)

A 44.45 mm (1.75 in) vertical increment used to specify equipment height in a 19-inch rack.

19 英寸机柜中 44.45 毫米的高度计量单位。

19-inch rack

The standardised equipment frame (EIA-310 / IEC 60297) with 482.6 mm front panel width.

标准化设备机架（EIA-310 / IEC 60297），前面板宽 482.6 毫米。

PDU

Power Distribution Unit: the rack-mounted unit that delivers and, in metered variants, monitors power to installed equipment.

机柜内配电单元，计量型还可监测各回路用电。

PUE

Power Usage Effectiveness: total facility power divided by IT equipment power; a standard data-center efficiency ratio.

电能使用效率，数据中心总能耗与 IT 设备能耗之比。

Structured cabling

A standards-based, organised cabling system of horizontal and backbone links, patching and administration.

按标准组织水平与主干链路、配线及管理的布线体系。

Blanking panel

A filler plate closing unused rack U-space to stop hot-air recirculation and protect cooling efficiency.

封堵机柜空余 U 位的挡板，防止热风回流、保障散热效率。

Busbar

A rigid conductive bar used to distribute high current within a rack or power distribution assembly.

机柜或配电组件内用于分配大电流的刚性导电排。

Bend radius

The minimum curvature allowed for a cable; exceeding it damages fibre or copper performance.

线缆允许的最小弯曲半径，过小会损伤光纤或铜缆性能。

TIA-942

The Telecommunications Infrastructure Standard for Data Centers covering cabling, spaces and topology.

面向数据中心电信基础设施（布线、空间、拓扑）的标准。

OCP

Open Compute Project: an open specification set for racks, power and server hardware published by the OCP community.

开放计算项目发布的机柜、供电与服务器硬件开放规范。
