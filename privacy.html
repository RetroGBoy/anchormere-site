2. What we collect

Inquiry form (via Formspree)

When you submit a quote request we receive: name, company, email, optional WhatsApp number, product line, target market, message, estimated quantity and timing. This is processed by Formspree to deliver the message to our sales inbox.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

With your consent, GA4 collects anonymized usage data — pages viewed, referrer, device/browser type, approximate region. No form data is mixed into analytics.

Direct communication

If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by Meta under its own privacy policy; we only keep what is needed to serve your inquiry.

Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies for site function and, after consent, analytics cookies. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner; see §6.

3. Legal basis & how we use data

  • To respond to your inquiry / prepare a quote — based on taking steps at your request before a contract (and the contract itself once a PO is placed).

  • We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.
