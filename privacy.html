Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data we collect through anchormere.com, why, and the rights you have. Last updated: 2026-10-28. This is a template — confirm it with qualified legal counsel before launch.

1. Who we are (data controller)

The site is operated by 深圳森亚实业有限公司 (Shenzhen Senya Industrial Co., Ltd.), your China sourcing partner based in the Greater Bay Area, Guangdong, China. For privacy requests, contact: info@anchormere.com · 中国广东深圳市光明区特发工业园B座.

2. What we collect

Inquiry form (direct email)

When you submit a quote request, your browser opens an email to info@anchormere.com containing: name, company, email, optional WhatsApp number, product line, target market, message, estimated quantity and timing. No third-party form processor is involved — the message is delivered straight to our sales inbox.

Analytics (optional, consent-based)

We do not load any third-party analytics by default. If you accept non-essential cookies via the banner, we may enable Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymized usage data — pages viewed, referrer, device/browser type, approximate region. No form data is mixed into analytics.

Direct communication

If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by Meta under its own privacy policy; we only keep what is needed to serve your inquiry.

Cookies

We use strictly-necessary cookies for site function and, after consent, analytics cookies. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner; see §6.

3. Legal basis & how we use data

  • To respond to your inquiry / prepare a quote — based on taking steps at your request before a contract (and the contract itself once a PO is placed).
  • Analytics & site improvement — based on your consent (non-essential cookies), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects.

4. Sharing & international transfer

We share data only as needed: with the specific Greater Bay Area workshop assigned to your purchase order (only the information required to fulfill it). The inquiry email is delivered directly to our own sales inbox — we do not route it through a third-party form processor. If you consent to analytics, anonymized usage data is processed by Google (a US-based processor); where data is transferred outside your region, processors rely on their certifications / standard contractual safeguards.

5. Retention

Inquiry records are kept only as long as needed to serve the relationship and meet legal/accounting obligations (typically up to 3 years for contract and accounting purposes). If analytics is enabled with your consent, that data is retained per Google's settings.

6. Cookies & your choices

A banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also clear cookies in your browser to reset the choice. Essential site cookies cannot be disabled as the site will not function without them.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including GDPR for EEA visitors, and similar regimes), you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or portability of your personal data, and object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email info@anchormere.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version. Material changes will be noted on the site.
